# Home · Three levels · design review

Light theme, 375px and 1280px. Phone measurements are CSS px (screenshot px ÷ 2). "≈" means measured from the screenshot, not from code.

## The five that matter most

1. **The part-changed notice contradicts the page under it (H1).** The notice says "Now: Invent your own" while the sticky now bar and the navy hero right below it still say "The Remix Arcade". So one viewport makes two different "now" claims and shows two copper buttons leading to two different places. On a phone the header, now bar and notice together take 268px, a third of the screen.
2. **The script is unreadable at 375px, the one place the page says to read before running (L1).** Soft-wrapped code drops its indentation and splits identifiers ("setFrozenRo / ws(1)", "vnd.google- / apps.document"). The level's own safety rule ("A line nobody can explain doesn't run") depends on people being able to read it.
3. **The front door (today's default) repeats itself instead of choosing a path (H4).** The hero bullets repeat step 1's examples, the hero button repeats step 1's link, the card's closing paragraph repeats the "Three levels" card, and the "Everything here" grid repeats the header nav. That grid also costs ≈960px on a phone, with four identical "Open →" links.
4. **Three levels shows the same three-way choice twice, and the second copy is clipped on a phone (L2, L3).** The three overview cards are followed at once by a 1·2·3 tab strip, so ≈900px of a phone screen goes to two navigations to the same places. At 375px the strip cuts its third tab to "Behin" with nothing to show it scrolls.
5. **On level 3, the safety guidance comes after the run (L8).** "A script acts with your account … read it before you run it" sits below Steps 1–7, below If it breaks, and below the "Rather have your AI write one?" fold. A reader following the page in order reads it after pressing Run and Allow.
