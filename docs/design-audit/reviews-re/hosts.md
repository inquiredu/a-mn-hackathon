# Hosts · design review

The packet names no ID prefix, so this review uses **HO** (H is taken by the brief's Home example). Image names are shortened. `after-375 s05` means `hosts/after-375-light-s05.png`. `open-375 s14` means `states/hosts-folds-open-375-light-s14.png`. Coordinates are CSS px: phone shots are halved from 2x, desktop shots are 1x.

## The five that matter most

1. **The run-of-show bar misstates the session and fails contrast (HO1, HO2).** Its segments look proportional but aren't. At 1280, "Three levels" (12 min) is 223px wide and "Invent your own" (40 min) is 169px; at 375 all seven are about 46px. The offset times on the teal breakout segments are #D7DEDE on #4D7887, which is 3.54:1 and fails AA.
2. **An open part is the host's main working surface, and its structure works against them (HO15, HO16).** Checklists and ready-to-paste messages come after all the screen notes, and by my count 22 of 27 checklist items repeat a note. At 375 the open Welcome part runs about 3,230px. At 1280 one part uses three different line lengths (550, 970, and 1000px).
3. **Hosts patch every blank by hand, and "Your links" looks like it would help but holds nothing (HO12).** The three rows under Materials look like links but are inert labels. Meanwhile the messages carry dashed "(your planner link)" chips that look editable but aren't. If those rows became three saved inputs that fill every blank, the most repeated chore of the day would go away.
4. **The top of the page doesn't change with the moment (HO3, HO4).** On any day the filled primary button is "Present the stage", and it appears three times (header, hero, Materials). The action a new host actually needs, "Start with Before the day", is a text link. During the event the hero stays the same, and the only route to the current part is a 19px-tall link in the now bar.
5. **There's no way to find your place on a page 13,520px long at 375 (28,044px with every part open) (HO6, HO7).** The ten "On this page" pills wrap to five rows (242px) and don't follow you down the page. At 1280 the right 403px beside the prose sits empty for about 4,000px of scrolling. The sand band marks an unnamed "on the day" chapter.
