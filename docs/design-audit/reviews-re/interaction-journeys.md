# Interaction and motion · Journeys across the site · design review

Image paths below are relative to `fb2/shots/`. "375" and "1280" are CSS widths; phone pixel positions are given in CSS px (half the screenshot's pixels).

## The five that matter most

1. **J1 (Break)**: The part-change notice contradicts the page under it. The notice says "Now: Invent your own" while the sticky now bar and the hero still say The Remix Arcade with "Next: Invent your own". It also puts two copper primaries in one view that point to different pages ("Start your invention" and "Open the starters"). This is the one moment a live participant most needs a single answer.
2. **I1 (Break)**: On a phone, Run my changes switches to The tool and shows a blank white frame with no message. The page's own instructions say a blank frame means "you lost a quote mark", so a working edit looks broken. At 1280 the same action reads correctly ("Running your version." beside the button).
3. **J2 (Friction, all three journeys)**: Every "Play a starter" and "Open the starters" lands at the top of Starters, about 2,045px (2.5 phone screens) above the first Play button. Between the two sit an audience box and four steps that repeat Home's three steps under new names. During the event the participant needs two taps for one intent ("Open the starters", then the now bar's "Jump to the starters").
4. **I2 (Break)**: Invent's empty-copy error at 375 is shown by copper borders alone. The three boxes already show example text that reads like answers, and the explaining sentence sits beside the button, about 100px below the bottom of the screen. Copper is also the focus-ring color, so an errored field that has focus is hard to read.
5. **J3 (Break)**: At the close, the big copper "Add your wonder and worry" button only jumps down to a card that says the link will be in the Meet chat. The label promises an action the page can't perform.

## Region by region

### Interaction and motion

- **Play window opening** (motion/play-open): The backdrop dims and the dialog fades and rises over about 160ms, with the frame fading in over 200ms; it's settled by 250ms. On spec, and at 1280 the near-full-bleed dialog (16px top and bottom) is right for showing a tool. Header button ranks: I5. Phone layout: I8.
- **Remix menu opening** (motion/remix-open, states/starters-remix-open-*): It opens within one frame and its contents fold in over 200ms, quiet and correct. Opening one card's menu at 1280 leaves the neighbor card about 500px shorter, a hole in the grid, which is acceptable for a disclosure. Copy flip width: I11. Blanks: J7.
- **Next-step panel** (motion/next-step, states/*next-step*): Arrival is a fade with a short rise, under 200ms, on spec. Its content is identical on Starters and Invent, which is good. Placement: I6. Content density: J6.
- **Toast** (motion/toast, states/*toast*): No toast is seen. I3.
- **Part-change notice** (motion/cue, states/home-cue-*): It fades in over 200ms, on spec. What it says against the rest of the page is the problem: J1.
- **In-page jumps** (motion/anchor-jump): No visible response. I9.
- **Done states**: "✓ Copied" (check, #E3EDF0 fill, navy border) is the same on Copy for my AI, remix Copy, and Copy my request, which is a good shared pattern. Width shift: I11.
- **Error state** (Invent): I2. **Selected state** (jar): I12. **Pressed toggle** (Show the code): I5. **Empty state** (Sources): the card names the query and offers one way out, good; the competing primary is I10.
- **Copy-by-hand dialog**: Instructions adapt to touch (press and hold) and keyboard (Ctrl+C), which is good. Sizing and ending: I7.
- **Play window, code and run**: At 1280, tool and code side by side with "Running your version." beside the button is the best interaction on the site. At 375 it breaks (I1), and Back to the original is mismatched and unguarded (I8).
- **Targets at 375**: Nav pills (36px), the theme toggle (36px), remix Copy (32px), tool tabs (40px), and Sources chips (32px) all clear 24px. Failures to verify: I4.
- **Focus**: Every measured element, in both themes, gets the 2px ring at a 3px offset. Its color is tuned per surface (#A9653A on cream, about 4:1; #D09466 on navy, about 6:1). Keep it. The only clash is with copper error borders (I2).
- **Hover and press**: Not verifiable from the inventory (I13). The stage's motion is slightly off spec (I14).
