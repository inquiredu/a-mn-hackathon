# Night (Home, Three levels, Starters) and the design system · design review

Image paths below are relative to `fb2/shots/`. Colors marked "sampled" were read from the screenshot pixels. Contrast ratios were computed from those samples.

**Verdict on Night.** Night mostly looks designed, not inverted. It has a clear four-step surface ladder (sampled): `#142434` for the header, footer and now bar; `#1A2E41` for the page; `#20384F` for cards; `#2E4B66` for tinted panels. Lighter means closer. Every text sample I measured passes AA. The lowest are muted `#C8D2DA` on a slate panel at 5.9:1 and copper text `#DCAD88` at 6.0 to 6.9:1. It reads inverted in five places, and those are the top findings below:
- one tint does five jobs;
- the caution loses its meaning;
- the darkest step is used for chrome, recesses, wells and the most important card alike;
- the cream fill means both "press me" and "you are here";
- copper is sprinkled rather than placed.

## The five that matter most

1. **X1 (Break).** In the Starters level 3 intro, "Copy the script" and "Copy for my AI" are drawn as buttons, but they are only illustrations. In Night the cream one is the brightest thing on the screen, and pressing it does nothing.
2. **X2.** One solid fill means both "primary action" and "selected". In Night the Play window puts two identical cream buttons side by side ("Hide the code" next to "Copy for my AI" at 1280; "Copy for my AI" above the selected "The tool" tab at 375). The header's "Home" pill is the same cream as the hero's "Play a starter". Give selected states their own treatment.
3. **D1, D2.** In Night, `#2E4B66` is the hairline color, the tinted-panel fill, the code-chip fill, the next-step sheet and the caution fill all at once. The Starters column dividers vanish (line on fill, both `#2E4B66`). Cautions and stop notes become identical to neutral notes except for a 4px copper rule, where Light keeps them warm (`#F7EBE0`). Split the tokens and give the caution a warm Night fill.
4. **D3.** Copper is sprinkled in Night. During the event, one 375px viewport carries six copper elements: two pills, two rules, copper text and two copper-filled buttons. That includes a copper-filled primary that exists nowhere else on the site. Copper also appears on LEVEL labels, 3 of 8 starter icons, the hero bullets and a full-bleed triple stripe. Set a copper budget.
5. **X4–X9.** The same job is done in several looks:
   - disclosures: 3 styles;
   - note and caution boxes: 5 styles;
   - sequence markers: 3 systems;
   - in-page navigation: 4 patterns;
   - static tiles that look like link cards;
   - type styles: 30 to 47 distinct per page, including six body sizes between 15.2 and 17.6px and seven letter-spacings on uppercase labels.

   The proposed system at the end needs 12 type levels, 10 components and 9 spacing steps.
