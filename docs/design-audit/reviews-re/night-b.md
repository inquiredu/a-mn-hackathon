# Night: Invent your own, Hosts, Sources · design review

The packet doesn't name a finding-ID prefix, so this review uses **NB** (Night, second half). Image names are relative to `scratchpad/fb2/shots/`. Phone measurements are in CSS px (the 2x images divided by two). Colors were sampled from the screenshots: page `#1a2e41`, card `#20384f`, raised panel `#2e4b66`, wells, chrome and the dark band `#142434`, cream `#f4f1eb`, secondary text `#c8d2da`, control borders `#9daebc`, Night copper `#dcad88`.

## The five that matter most

1. **NB1 · The timeline's breakout segments fail AA.** The labels on the Run of show bar ("0:28", "0:45") measure 3.5:1 on the teal (`#d7dede` on `#4d7887`). Even pure cream would only reach 4.3:1. The labels are about 13px, so they need 4.5:1.
2. **NB2 · The jar clips the third answer on a phone.** Pressing an idea fills "What should it do?" with three lines, but the textarea shows two and cuts the third off mid-line. The fill looks broken at exactly the moment it should feel like magic.
3. **NB3 · Cream fill means everything in Night.** The same cream fill marks the primary action, the current nav page, the selected tab, the selected AI tab, the active filter chip, the active topic pill, the main-room timeline segments and the part-card stripes. So no view has one clear primary action. On Sources at 375, the loudest thing on screen is a selected tab, not something to press.
4. **NB4 · The builder's example placeholders read as answers.** "a countdown timer" (`#9daebc`) sits in the same face and size as a typed answer (`#f4f1eb`) on the near-black well. The fields look filled, so people press Copy and get the empty-fields error. The fields then turn copper with the examples still showing in them.
5. **NB5 · Night's surfaces don't keep their roles.** The raised tone `#2e4b66` serves aside panels, copy-this boxes, message cards, tags, the selected jar idea and both overlays. So the help panels outrank the builder, and the copied sheet and popover blend into the request box under them. A prompt box inside "Stuck?" also loses its fill entirely. Meanwhile the recessed tone `#142434` serves wells, the header, the footer and whole content bands, so the footer has no edge on Sources and Invent.

## Region by region

**Invent your own**

- **Hero:** Eyebrow, H1 and lede are clean. The lede is six lines at 375, but the first field still lands inside the first screen. Fine.
