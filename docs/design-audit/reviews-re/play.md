# The Play window and the six starters · design review

The packet names no finding-ID prefix, so this review uses **P**. Phone measurements are in CSS px: the 2x screenshot pixels divided by two.

## The five that matter most

1. **P1 (Break).** On a phone, pressing Run my changes switches to The tool and shows a plain white frame with no message, in both themes (the four `play-*-run-toast-375-*` shots). The help text just said that a blank frame means the code broke, so a run that worked looks exactly like one that failed. The desktop shows "Running your version." beside the button; the phone shows nothing.
2. **P2 (Break).** The code box doesn't wrap. At 375 every line that holds a value someone is meant to change is cut off at the right edge (`minutes: 20` is hidden, so is the text of the norms). At 1280 the comment and norms lines are cut too. Changing a number shouldn't require scrolling sideways inside a box that also scrolls up and down.
3. **P4 (Friction).** Selected and toggled states use the same solid fill as the primary button. On a phone the selected "The tool" segment is the same solid navy slab as Copy for my AI, directly below it. On desktop, Hide the code turns solid, so the code view has three equal solid buttons (Hide the code, Copy for my AI, Run my changes). Phone and desktop also use two different controls for the same switch.
4. **P6 (Friction, with P3 Break).** The tool's frame has no consistent edge. In light it runs full-bleed with no border, so a cream starter (Group Maker) blends into the dialog and its title appears twice. In Night it is inset 9px with rounded corners, and on desktop that inset clips the Oracle's privacy line at the bottom of the frame (P3).
5. **P5 (Friction).** Nothing says you're looking at your own version. After Run my changes the header still reads "PLAYING The Meeting Timer", and Copy for my AI doesn't say whether it copies your edits or the original. Edit, run, copy is the whole remix loop, and its state is invisible.

## Region by region

- **Play window · phone header** (all play-*-375-*): the eyebrow, title, and Close row is clear, and Close is a 75×43 target. But three stacked control rows cost 185px (P7). The full-width Copy for my AI is the strongest shape on screen, which is right in the tool view, except that the selected segment directly under it matches it (P4).
- **Play window · desktop header** (play-*-1280-*): an 86px cream (Night: navy) bar with a hairline, title on the left, three 44px buttons on the right. Its proportions are good. Close needs separating (P11), and the toggled state must not turn solid (P4).
- **View switch**: a segmented control "The tool / The code" on phones, a "Show the code / Hide the code" button on desktop. Same job, two components, two sets of labels, and both mark "on" with a primary-style fill (P4). The width where one becomes the other isn't visible (see questions).
- **Frame**: in light it has no edge, and in Night it's inset 9px with rounded corners and a hairline (P6). The Night inset clips the Oracle (P3). On a phone after Run, it's a bare white panel (P1). Text inside may be soft at 375 (P13).
- **Code pane · help text**: one dense paragraph mixing how to edit and how to recover (P8).
- **Code pane · textarea**: no wrap, so values are clipped (P2), and it opens on boilerplate (P10). In Night it's well made: a deeper navy box with cream mono text that reads well.
- **Code pane · button row and confirmation**: the buttons are mismatched heights (P9). "Running your version." on desktop is the right idea in the right place, but small, possibly temporary, and missing on phones (P1, P5).
- **Night theme overall**: the chrome inverts properly (cream primary on navy), and the starters stay light, as their own looks. That's acceptable, but it makes the blank-frame state in P1 a bright white slab, and the inset frame belongs to Night only (P6).
- **Meeting Timer**: a strong NOW card with big digits and a clear agenda with a current-item rule. Its problems: Chime competes as a second primary and its label is ambiguous (P14); the controls appear in three arrangements with "+1 minute" orphaned (P15); running barely looks different from paused (P16); the phone header wraps and the parking lot shows no destination (P17). The norms bar at 375 becomes a three-line run-on inside a box; since the norms are a short list, consider showing them as a list with dot separators replaced by line breaks under 480px.
- **Group Maker**: it opens with groups already made (good), and the group cards are well proportioned at every width. Shuffle is buried on phones (P18), the rust is overused and the count line is ambiguous (P19), and the change has no cue (P25). At 960 and 1280 the left input card runs about 200px past the right column's end; that's acceptable, but if the Spread-out field moved into a collapsed "Options" row, the two columns would end level.
- **Feedback Builder**: the best-resolved starter. Clear rubric rows, filled-pill selection that doesn't depend on colour alone (fill versus outline), live feedback, and the phone's sticky "Feedback ready / updated. See it ↓" bar. Action placement needs work (P20), and the update deserves a cue on desktop (P25). At 375 the 2×2 level grids make the page 1841px long. That's acceptable; tightening the row gap from about 12px to 8px and the criterion gap from about 24px to 20px would save about 100px without crowding.
- **Teacher's Lounge Oracle**: the most distinctive look: a well-drawn illustration, a "You asked:" echo, and gold answer type. The Play-frame layout is the weaker one (P21), particles sit over the input (P22), and the sound toggle has the emoji and wording issue (P14). On phones the band reserved for answers ("The orb is listening.") holds about 130px of empty space, then still shifts 17px when an answer arrives. Size it to a two-line answer (about 64px) and let longer answers grow it.
- **Charter Cards**: a calm, readable accordion with a visible open state. Its pills are unlabeled and could pass for buttons, and it disagrees with its twin (P23).
- **Syllabus Page**: good section eyebrows and a month column that works at 960. Its open state is weaker than Charter's (P23), and the phone layout is squeezed by the month column (P24).
- **The set**: six distinct palettes, with the same idioms for pill buttons, solid primaries, outlined secondaries, and a small "made-up" footnote under each. That consistency teaches remixing. Five starters share the same system sans and the Oracle uses a serif, which is fine. The weak point is Charter and Syllabus: one pattern, two treatments (P23). As a pair they also show less range than the other four (see questions).
- **The whole, eye path**: on a phone, the eye goes to the navy Copy slab, then the equally navy selected segment, then the tool. With P4 and P7 that becomes title, then the tool, then Copy, which is the order Play, Copy, Remix wants. On desktop in the tool view, the eye goes tool, then Copy, which is right. In the desktop code view, three equal solids split attention (P4). Spacing inside the phone header is even (11–12px between rows), and the dialog's 14px phone gutter is close to the site's 16px. Copper doesn't appear in the window at all, which is correct for a surface whose content is someone else's palette.

## Keep

- A full-screen sheet on phones, and a large dialog with 16px margins on desktop: the tool gets the room.
- In the desktop tool view, exactly one solid button, Copy for my AI.
- The desktop split: the live tool beside its code, with the confirmation inline next to Run my changes.
- Back to the original always visible beside Run my changes, so recovery is one press away.
- Night chrome with an inverted cream primary, and the deeper navy code box with cream mono text.
- Every starter opens showing its output: groups made, feedback composed, the first card open, the agenda loaded.
- The Feedback Builder's sticky "Feedback ready / updated. See it ↓" bar on phones. Reuse it in the Group Maker (P18).
- Each starter's own palette, the shared pill and primary idioms, and the one-line "made-up" footnote.
- The Oracle's illustration, the "You asked:" echo, and the gold answer type.

## Questions for the owner

- After Run my changes, does Copy for my AI copy the edited code or the original? The label should say which (P5).
- Were the blank phone frames after Run captured before the frame finished loading, or does the phone build skip the confirmation? (P1 needs a designed state either way.)
- Should the starters follow a visitor's dark preference (`prefers-color-scheme`) in their own palettes? They would look right in Night and in AI tools' dark preview panels, but every remixer would see about 15 more lines of CSS.
- Charter Cards and Syllabus Page share one pattern. Are they meant as a pair (department and classroom), or would a different pattern for one of them better show how far a remix can go?
- At what width does the phone's segmented control give way to the desktop's Show the code button? It isn't visible in the packet, and P4 proposes making them one control.
