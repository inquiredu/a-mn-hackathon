# Sources · design review

Light theme, 375px and 1280px, default ("after"), during-event, path chosen, topic filter, filters panel, and no-results states. The packet names no ID prefix, so findings use **SRC**. Shot names below drop the `sources/` and `states/` folders; `s05` means slice 5. Page lengths: 20,842px at 375 (about 30 phone screens) and 15,428px at 1280 (about 21 laptop screens).

## The five that matter most
1. **The phone's "Search and filter" toggle is drawn as a text field** (same border, fill, radius, height and left-aligned text as the real search input, which appears right below it once opened). People tap it to type and get a 1,000px panel instead. (SRC1)
2. **The filters scroll away and never come back.** At 1280 the sidebar ends about 2,680px down. For the remaining 12,400px (17 of 23 slices) the left third of the screen is empty sand. On a phone there is no sticky control, group index, or way back up through about 25 screens of cards. (SRC2)
3. **Two overlapping taxonomies, five ways in.** The tabs filter by group ("Proceed with care 13", "Does coding help thinking? 12") and the sidebar filters by near-twin topic groups ("Taking care", "Coding and thinking 8"). On top of those come kinds, search, six Start-here paths, and two "See the sources on…" buttons, all for 45 items. The counts disagree, and combining controls produces empty tabs. (SRC3)
4. **Each source card is seven rows tall** (type eyebrow, title, meta, note, actions, hairline, "Topics" plus pills), 300–360px on a phone. That card height is why the page runs 30 screens. Merging the type into the meta line and dropping the "Topics" label and its hairline saves about four phone screens. (SRC5)
5. **A chosen path shows nothing.** Both `?path=leaders` captures are pixel-identical to the default page top: no banner, no selected path card, no scroll. A shared "for leaders" link looks like it did nothing. (SRC4)

## Region by region
- **Header** (header-sources-375-light, header-sources-1280-light): At 1280 the cream "Sources" pill on navy is a clear you-are-here marker, and the Present/GitHub/theme cluster is set apart by a divider. At 375 the collapsed bar ("First Builds", "Menu ⌄", a 44px target) doesn't say which page you're on, but the H1 does. Nothing here needs to change for this page.
- **Now bar** (during-375-light-s01, during-1280-light-s01): A good cross-link on a reading page. At 1280 it fits one line ("NOW · The Remix Arcade · Open the starters · Next: Invent your own"). At 375 the second line starts with a stray "·" and "Next" is dropped (SRC13). It adds 66px on a phone, which is acceptable.
- **Hero**: The eyebrow (the checked date) and the H1 are right. The lede has a good measure (about 720px at 1280). The two cross-links overpower the page's own way in on a phone (SRC6). The hairline under them stops at 761px, matching neither the lede (838) nor the container (1164) (SRC14).
- **How to read a card**: In the wrong place and too faint (SRC10).
- **The short version**: The structure is right: three bold run-in leads, each a claim with its hedge. At 1280 the box is far wider than its text (SRC12). It sits 16px from a box with a different treatment (SRC12).
- **How the session is built on this**: The densest block on the page, a single 14-line paragraph at 375 with five citations (SRC6). Its two outline buttons repeat the path cards' job in a different form, stacked ragged on a phone (SRC17).
- **Start here (path cards)**: The page's best idea, and it sits too low (SRC6). Description lengths are uneven, and the tight slate style is a third body style (SRC16). "AI?" is orphaned (SRC13). "Read these N →" says exactly what it does: keep it. Whole-card targeting and hover are unconfirmed (SRC16).
- **Browse band**: The sand band separating browse from intro is a good, quiet section change. The tabs are the second taxonomy (SRC3). At 1280 their width aligns with nothing (SRC14). At 375 they become a four-row list (176px), a designed phone treatment, but a filter people will confuse with topics.
- **Search and filters**: On the phone, the toggle mimics an input (SRC1) and the panel is longer than the screen with its apply button out of reach (SRC24). The desktop sidebar is well grouped: labelled search, single-choice topics under three eyebrow groups, multi-choice kinds, right-aligned live counts, and 32px rows. It scrolls away (SRC2). "Law, policy, and standards" runs to within about 8px of its count; give the count column a fixed 32px.
- **Results header**: "All 45 sources." / "Showing 5 of 45 sources." with the On: chips is the right content. It is glued to the first group (SRC11), and its labels don't match the cards (SRC9).
- **Group and subgroup headings**: The scale collapses on a phone, and the two stacked eyebrows look alike (SRC8). Group intros are muted (SRC18). One group and its first subgroup echo each other (SRC23). The hairline-plus-96px break before "Proceed with care" and "Does coding help thinking?" is the right rhythm; give the first group the same.
- **Source card**: The title with ↗, the meta line with an "as of" date, and the bold "For the session:" / "The habit:" lead are a strong core. Everything around that core costs height (SRC5). Meta and title line breaks look unset (SRC13). The action labels are vague (SRC9). Pill behaviour is unsaid, and wrapped pills lose their indent (SRC19). Desktop padding is lopsided (SRC15). The preprint is styled like peer-reviewed work (SRC22).
- **Filtered state** (sources-filter-375-light, sources-filter-1280-light): The navy-filled matched pill shows why each card is there, which is excellent. Live tab counts are good. A tab at 0 stays in the row (dimmed at 1280) (SRC7). The "Clear" controls overlap (SRC7).
- **Empty state** (sources-no-results-375-light, sources-no-results-1280-light): Echoing the query ("No source mentions 'zzzz'.") with one primary "Clear the search" is right. The panel's "Show 0 sources", the disabled "Open every summary", and the undimmed 0-count options undercut it (SRC7). It could also offer the three group names as fallbacks, as text links.
- **Path chosen state**: Nothing visible (SRC4).
- **Footer**: The partner mark leads, the safety and blocked-site notes are useful, and the author mark and licence sit at the lower left as specified. The external-link glyph differs from the rest of the page (SRC20). The provenance caveat belongs near the top on this page (SRC21).
- **The whole**: At 1280 the eye goes from the H1 to the large tinted short-version panel to the lede. At 375 it goes from the H1 to the lede to two bold exits. The intended order (what this is, where to start, then browse) arrives late on both. The hero has no primary action of its own; Browse has one (filters); the list rightly has none. Spacing is mostly systematic: 12px between cards, about 36/18px around subgroup labels, about 96px plus a hairline between groups. The exceptions are the 16px between the intro boxes, the 10–20px between the results header and the first group, and the missing hairline before the first group. Left edges are true at both widths; right edges vary (SRC14, SRC15). The type scale holds at 1280 and collapses at 375 (SRC8). Copper is effectively absent in the light default (the footer beaver and the during-event NOW pill only); state is carried by navy fills, consistently, which is fine. Clickability is mostly honest, with three exceptions: the input-shaped toggle (SRC1), pills whose effect is unsaid (SRC19), and the path card body (SRC16, inferred). On the phone, the controls feel designed (stacked tabs, a collapsible panel), while the cards feel squeezed: wrapped meta, wrapped pills, half-screen cards. A first-time visitor's likeliest misreads: the toggle as a search box, the tabs and topics as the same thing, "Show 0 sources" as a way forward, and "Copy link" as copying the page.

## Keep
- The "Checked October 7 and 8, 2026" eyebrow and the per-card "as of" / "updated" dates: provenance on show.
- The bold run-in leads ("For the session:", "The habit:", "Later, not for the session:"). They make the payload of each card scannable.
- The navy-filled matched pill in filtered results.
- Live counts on tabs and options, and the "Search and filter · 1 on" summary on the closed phone toggle.
- The empty state's query echo with one recovery button.
- The two-column browse at desktop; the collapsible panel and stacked tab list on the phone.
- The sand band that separates browse from the introduction.
- Balanced wrapping on source titles, and the ↗ on every external title.
- The three-point structure of "The short version".
- "Read these N →" as a link label: it says what pressing does, and how much.

## Questions for the owner
- The packet names no finding-ID prefix; I used SRC. Rename if it collides.
- Should `?path=` scroll to the results on load, and what should a chosen path look like? The captures show nothing, so SRC4 is partly inferred.
- Do the four tabs need to filter, or can they become a section index (SRC3)? If they must filter, the topic group labels need names that don't echo them. That is a wording call.
- What does "Copy link" copy: the source's URL, or a link to that card on this page?
- Is the "Help pages for AI tools change often…" note site-wide or written for Sources (SRC21)?
- Should the one preprint get a distinct treatment, and is dark copper acceptable for it (SRC22)?
