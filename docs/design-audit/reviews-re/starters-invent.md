# Starters · Invent your own · design review

Light theme, 375px and 1280px. All positions are CSS px: page offset = the slice's scroll value + (image y ÷ 2) on phone slices, + image y on desktop slices.

## The five that matter most

1. **V1 (Break):** On Invent, the example text inside the three builder fields looks like answers someone already typed. It uses the same Montserrat 16px as typed text and is only slightly lighter. People will press Copy on what they think is a filled form, and the empty-copy state proves the confusion: three fields that look filled get flagged as empty.
2. **V2 (Break):** At 375px, pressing Copy with nothing filled scrolls the page to three copper-outlined fields. The words that explain why ("Answer the three questions first…") sit below the button, out of view. On a phone the only signal is color (WCAG 1.4.1). At 1280px the message is in a different column from the fields it's about.
3. **S5 (Break):** The Level 3 intro shows a filled "Copy the script" button and an outlined "Copy for my AI" button as a key to the card buttons. They're smaller (31px tall, against 43px on the cards) and sit before any card. If they do nothing, they're buttons that lie. If they work, there's no telling which of the two scripts they copy.
4. **S1 (Friction):** On Starters, no starter is visible in the first screen at either width. On a phone the first Play button is at ≈2,045px, about 2.5 screens down. Before it come the level pills, a 420px box answering three questions, a 610px four-step how-to, and an empty band between two rules. At 1280px the first card starts at 1,055px, below the fold.
5. **S7 (Friction):** At 1280px, cards in the same row have different heights, so Play buttons don't line up and holes open up: about 118px of blank space under the Meeting Timer. Opening one Remix menu stretches that card about 650px while its neighbor stays put, leaving about 500px of empty cream and pushing the next row off screen.
