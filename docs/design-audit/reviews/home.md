# Home · design review

Scope: Home in its after, before and during states (remix, cheer, close), plus the notice that appears when the part changes. Light theme, 375px and 1280px. Measurements are in CSS px; I halved the phone figures from the 2x images.

## The five that matter most
1. **H1**: The part-changed notice says "Now: Grant a wish." It sits between the now bar and the Happening card, and both of those still say the Remix Arcade is on. That gives three "now" signals that disagree, and two primary buttons that go to different parts. That is a misleading state at exactly the moment people need direction.
2. **H3**: The after hero, which every visitor sees today, opens with a 9-line lede and three buttons of near-equal weight. The first step card starts at ≈1,108px on a phone and ≈1,048px at 1280, so it is below the fold at both widths. At 1280 the right ≈470px of the hero is empty.
3. **H8**: During the event, the current part's own section (the awards, the close) sits below the whole schedule. On a phone that is ≈1,730px of scrolling for Show & Cheer and ≈2,560px for the close, past rows that are finished but still full height.
4. **H6**: "Run it with your own team" is one ≈190-word paragraph: 21 lines at 375 and a ≈770px-tall card. It is the only door for hosts, and it won't be read as it stands.
5. **H4**: The after page sends people to Hosts four ways and to Three levels four ways. "Everything here" repeats the nav as ≈1,270px of stacked cards on a phone, and at 1280 its 3+2 grid leaves an empty sixth cell.

## Region by region
- **Header (all states)**: At 1280 it is clean and compact (57px), and the current pill reads as selected. At 375 the clipped scroller hides half the site (H14). "Present" and "GitHub" don't belong in a participant's primary nav (H15). The repeated wordmark costs phone space (H26).
- **Now bar (before, during)**: The right idea: sticky, single-line at 1280, with the pill doing the work. It wraps and strands separators on phones (H21). It must never disagree with the notice (H1).
- **Part-changed notice**: Offering a choice ("Take me there" / "Got it") is the right pattern. It contradicts the page it sits on (H1), and its buttons likely fail the focus-ring contrast check (H2). "Take me there" doesn't name where (H13).
- **Hero, after**: The type is strong. The lede is too long, the actions are split three ways, and the steps are below the fold. At 1280 the right half is empty (H3). The hairline is misaligned (H27). The partner credit block is well drawn.
- **Hero, before**: Date line plus lede is a good invitation. There is no primary action, and the join instruction is the quietest text (H10). The four "shape" figures belong here (H11).
- **Stripes**: The one bold graphic. It lands on the fold at 1280 in the before state (H16). It is absent in during states, which I think is right, since the navy card carries the weight there.
- **On your own? Start here (after)**: The best section on the page: three steps, equal-height cards at 1280, links pinned to the card foot. The badges look like buttons (H24). The trailing "Want more" line is an orphan that duplicates a card below (H4).
- **Everything here**: Restates the nav. It is long on phones, has an orphan cell at 1280, and its "Open" links are weak (H4, H5).
- **Run it with your own team / Show one person band**: Right audience, wrong structure: a wall of text (H6). The stretched sibling card leaves dead space (H20).
- **Happening card (during)**: The correct loudest element during the event. It needs a second button for Levels 2–3, time left, and use of its right half (H7). "Next" punctuation (H22).
- **The morning (schedule)**: Lengths-only and the copper-bordered current row are clear. Columns are too narrow on phones and lines too long at 1280 (H12). Past rows don't recede and use dashed borders (H9). The current part's own section comes after the whole list (H8). Labels mix verbs, nouns and destinations (H13).
- **Before you come**: The bold-lead-in list is the model for H6 and H18. It sits too late in the order (H11).
- **The shape of the morning**: Four cards for four numbers; better as one row in the hero (H11).
- **Show & Cheer section**: Clear intro. The award cards are tall and their tiles look like buttons (H23). It should sit directly under the Happening card while it is on, and disappear afterwards (H8).
- **What did we just do?**: The three parallel parts are broken into 2 cards + a line, and the where-to-post lines are detached from their prompts. Item 4 reads as disabled (H18).
- **Footer**: Credits and the signature are correct and quiet. The notes line is too long at 1280 (H25).

## Keep
- The navy Happening card as the single loudest element during the event, with one copper CTA on it.
- A sticky now bar with a pill (SOON / NOW) and the part name; at 1280 it is exactly one line.
- Lengths, not clock times, in the schedule. The current row marked with a copper border and an in-row NOW pill.
- At 1280, the three step cards with equal heights and links pinned to the foot.
- The "Before you come" pattern (bold lead-in + one or two sentences) as the house structure for dense blocks.
- Montserrat headings over Newsreader body at generous leading. The large H1 and the wide-tracked eyebrows.
- The footer's order: partner first, safety and fallback notes, then the author's quiet signature.
- The notice's offer-don't-force pattern (go, or dismiss), once it agrees with the page.
