# Hosts · design review

Measurements are in CSS px (phone shots halved from 2x). "1280" and "375" name the width; "after", "during", and "folds-open" name the packet's states.

## The five that matter most

1. **O1 (Break).** At 375 the whole run of show is wider than the screen. Every part card is clipped on the right, mid-word, and the Show/Hide control is off-screen. Long Google Slides URLs in two messages don't wrap and stretch the list. The page's core tool can't be read or opened on a phone.
2. **O2 (Break).** The Materials empty states ("Your Meet: paste its link…" and two more) are inline italic text flowing among the buttons. At 1280 they sit beside a button off its baseline and run into each other. At 375 they read as footnotes. The one instruction a host most needs here looks like stray text.
3. **O4 + O5 (Friction).** During the session the host's current part sits about 7,400px down on a phone (about 4,000px at 1280). The now bar offers the participant's link, not "go to this part". A 13,600px reference page has no in-page index at all.
4. **O6 (Friction).** The instructions people act on are set as walls of text: a 22-line card, a 20-line bullet with inline (1)(2)(3) steps, a 13-line intro. These need summary lines, sub-lists, and definition lists, or the steps will be missed.
5. **O10 (Friction).** Inside each part, the stage-screen list is numbered across the session (a list starting at "7."). It has 21 bold copper-underlined titles, so copper becomes the run of show's main color. Its "Say / Do / Co-host" cues are buried in 14px prose at about 165 characters a line. This is the panel a presenter glances at live.

## Region by region

- **Header (header-hosts-375/1280):** At 1280 it's clean. The current page is a cream pill, and "Present" is an outlined pill, the one header action. At 375 the current page is off-screen and the row's scroll is hinted only by a clipped word (O3).
- **Now bar (during-375/1280 s01):** The copper NOW pill, part name, link, and "Next" are compact and legible, and wrap to two lines at 375. On Hosts the link should serve the host (O4).
- **Hero (after-375 s01, after-1280 s01):** The eyebrow and H1 are well set. The lead is long, muted, and has no action. The page's summary figures live 2,000–3,000px further down (O16). Add the index under it (O5).
- **Two ways to run it (after-1280 s01–s02, after-375 s01–s03):** The right idea, a fork in the road, but set as unequal text cards with no actions (O6, O20).
- **Who you need (after-375 s03):** Bold lead-ins work. The co-host bullet (9 lines at 375) could split into "Before" and "During" sub-bullets.
- **Before the day (after-375 s03–s06):** The bold lead-ins work. The bullets are too long (O6). The inline code chip breaks across lines (O19). The invitation box is detached from its bullet and too wide (O14).
- **Materials (after-1280 s04, after-375 s07):** The single filled primary is right. The empty link slots break the layout (O2).
- **Using the stage (after-375 s08):** Good content, wrong format for keys (O24). The stat cards glued under it belong in the hero (O16).
- **Forming teams band (after-375 s09–s11, after-1280 s05–s06):** A good numbered sequence with bold "when" lead-ins. The calculator card is crowded against the list and under-weights its answer (O22).
- **Run of show (after-375 s11–s15, after-1280 s06–s09, all folds-open slices):** The strongest idea on the site: one card per part with stage, checklist, and paste-ready messages. It needs the overflow fix (O1), a scannable timeline (O7), clearer disclosure and aligned titles (O8), closed by default (O9), a calmer stage list (O10), marked blanks (O11), Copy feedback (O12), tidier message boxes (O13), and visible progress (O25).
- **In the rooms, and after (after-1280 s10–s12, after-375 s15–s19):** Three solid cards, but "When it breaks" is orphaned and buried (O17), and the stretched cards leave blank bottoms.
- **How the first run went (after-375 s19–s20, folds-open-375 s26–s28):** A sound record with links to the view-only files. The disclosure style and the license paragraph under it mislead (O18).
- **Footer (after-375 s20, after-1280 s12):** The credits are fixed and correct. The "Site blocked" line repeats Materials (O27); leave it, it's sitewide.
- **The whole:** At 1280 the eye lands on the H1, then on the dense left card, and never on an action. There's no primary action above the fold except the header's Present pill. Copper is sprinkled as dozens of link underlines rather than one highlight per view (O10, O23). The page uses about eight widths and four gap sizes (O15, O21). The phone layout is the desktop layout squeezed; in the run of show it breaks (O1).

## Keep

- One filled navy primary ("Present the stage") with outlined secondaries.
- Part cards that lead with the duration, carry a room pill, and use a navy or Water left bar to separate main room from breakout.
- The ready-to-paste box with a "TO: MAIN CHAT" / "TO: EACH BREAKOUT ROOM" eyebrow: naming the recipient is exactly right.
- Checklists inside each part, next to the messages they pair with.
- Bold-problem, plain-fix pairs in "When it breaks"; bold lead-ins in "Who you need" and "Before the day".
- The room calculator's plain answer ("8 rooms: 6 rooms of 4 and 2 rooms of 3.").
- The first-run record kept as a fold, with links to its view-only files.

## Questions for the owner

- Will a co-host use this page on a phone during the live session, or mostly on a laptop beside the stage? That sets how far to push O4, O5, and the phone layout of the run of show.
- Do the screen numbers (7–21) match anything shown on the stage or in the notes? If not, should they be dropped rather than labeled (O10)?
- On non-event days, should every part start closed, or should the page reopen the last part this browser opened (O9)?
- Should the license note live on Hosts at all, or only in the README the "Your own copy" card links to (O18)?
