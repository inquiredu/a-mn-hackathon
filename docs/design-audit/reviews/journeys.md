# Journeys across the site · design review

Paths below are relative to `audit/shots/`. Phone captures are 2x, so every phone measurement here is in CSS px (the image's pixels divided by 2). Desktop captures are 1x.

## The five that matter most

1. **The stage puts presenter chrome on the slide that participants see (J13, J14).** On all five stage captures, the keyboard-hint pill sits on top of the presenter controls, and on screens 3 and 4 it and the controls lie across the slide title. The stage tab is what gets shared in Meet, so every participant sees the collision.
2. **Screen 1 draws the session clock on top of its own orientation text (J15).** "2:00:00" overlaps the small print that tells a first-time host what this page is and where the hosts' guide is, and that link sits under the clock's digits. It's the first thing a coach sees after pressing Present.
3. **The part-changed notice contradicts its own page (J19).** The notice says "Now: Grant a wish". Directly above it the now bar still says "NOW The Remix Arcade", and directly below it the hero still says "Happening now · The Remix Arcade". That's three answers to "what's on now" in one phone screen.
4. **On a phone, the first starter sits about 3,000px (four screens, about 550 words) below the top of Starters (J3).** Home has already taught the same steps. A teacher who came to try a starter reads the paste instructions twice before seeing one.
5. **Running it takes about 1,000 words and 2,300px to reach, and there's no single way to open the stage and the notes together (J9, J12).** The facts that answer "what does it take" (2 hours, 57 minutes, rooms of 4, 7 parts) sit about 2,990px down Hosts, after the setup prose. Opening the two windows is described three different ways on three surfaces, and no single press does it.

## Region by region

### Journey 1 · Teacher on a phone, any day: try a starter in their own AI chat

Path as built: Home, then Try a starter (or Start here, then "Open the starters"), then Starters, about 3,000px of scrolling, then Play, Copy for my AI, Open Gemini, choose Canvas, paste. That's about 6 presses and 550–800 words of reading before the first paste.

- **Step 1 · Home hero (home/after-375-light-s01)**: The H1 and eyebrow say what the site is. Below them, 9 lines of reading come before any button, then three buttons with no single primary (J1), and the nav strip is cut at the edge (J25). The partner credit and the triple copper stripe sit between the buttons and the steps. That's fixed, but it pushes "Start here" to the second screen, which makes trimming J1 more important.
- **Step 2 · "On your own? Start here" (home s02–s03)**: The best part of this journey: three short step cards, each with one link out. It assumes a laptop (J2). Step 1's link should land on the first starter, not the top of Starters (J3d). The aside "Want more than the chat? The three levels…" runs narrower than the cards, and about 140px of empty space follows it before "Everything here". That's single-page polish, but it reads as the page ending.
- **Step 2b · "Everything here" (home s04–s05)**: Five cards whose only label is "Open", in a link style found nowhere else (J27). A teacher who already chose doesn't need it, and a wanderer can't tell what's clickable.
- **Step 3 · Starters top (starters s01–s04)**: The intro, the three-audience box, four step cards and the paste guide all come before any starter (J3). The four step cards restart the numbering and restate Home's steps in a different style (J26). The paste guide's nested cards inside a copper-ruled card make a third level of containment.
- **Step 4 · Starter card (states/starters-next-step, top)**: The icon tile, audience eyebrow, title, one-line value and "Monday:" note are clear. The panel hides the card's buttons, so I can't judge them here (J6).
- **Step 5 · Play window (states/play-timer)**: The starter is clipped on the right (J4). The header gives three equal buttons over two rows, and Close isn't where people expect it (J5). The starter's own look (system sans, teal Start, a bell emoji) is a visible change of world. That's acceptable for a standalone tool, but the dialog chrome should frame it clearly as "a preview".
- **Step 6 · Next-step panel (states/starters-next-step)**: The right idea: per-tool buttons with one-line hints at the moment of need. It covers the card, merges confirmation with question, and stops at Done (J6). The tool order differs from Home (J26).
- **Step 7 · Grant a wish, next (wish s01–s03)**: An 11-line intro and a dead "Start here" pointer (J7). The builder's live preview is good. "Copy my wish" doesn't appear to lead into the panel (J28). Step 2 restates the paste help a third time and links back to Starters' guide (J26). The numbering restarts at 1 again.
