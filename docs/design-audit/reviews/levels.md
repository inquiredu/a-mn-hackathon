# Three levels · design review

Phone measurements are in CSS px (the screenshots are 2x). Page positions come from the slice offsets in the packet.

## The five that matter most
1. **L2 (Break).** At 375, opening a fold breaks it. Each prompt pours into a column about 96px wide, one to three words per line, for about 1,100px (Level 2) and 1,200px (Level 3), while the right two-thirds of the box stays empty. Opening "Read the script" gives about 570px of blank tint with no code in it. The safety box says "Ours is short enough to read on this page", and on a phone that promise fails.
2. **L1 (Break).** At 375, the fold header rows lay the summary and the copy buttons side by side. "Read the prompt" wraps to three lines. In the script fold, "Copy the script" sits on top of the word "Read", and "Copy for my AI" runs past the fold box, the card, and the right edge of the screen.
3. **L3 (Break).** At 1280, the open script is cut off at the button column ("…a new Google Shee", "setValues(rows", "sheet.getU") with about 335px of empty tint beside it. It is also cut off at the bottom with no cue that more lines exist, so the script can't be read in full on a laptop either.
4. **L4 (Friction).** The page is 11,353px long at 375, about 14 phone screens. Level 2 starts near 3,600px, Level 3 near 5,870px, and the Level 3 card alone is about 4,100px. Past the chooser there is no in-page navigation: no way to switch levels, no way back, and each level's heading scrolls out of sight.
5. **L6 (Friction).** Each level's key action sits away from the step that names it. Level 2's step 1 says "Press Copy the prompt below", but the button is about 710px further down, after step 5. Every copy button is an outline button, so no level has one clear primary action.

## Region by region
- **Header**: The wordmark, nav, and current-page pill read clearly at 1280, and the 1120px grid lines up from the wordmark down to the footer (x=116). At 375 the sideways-scrolling nav hides half the site (L20). The current-page pill and the Present pill are two pill styles side by side that compete.
- **Now bar (during)**: The NOW pill reads well (navy on light copper, 7.8:1). At 375 it wraps to two lines (about 70px). "The three levels" is underlined like a link and probably points to the page you're already on (inferred). The page beneath it doesn't change for the event (L16).
- **Hero**: The h1 sets a clear scale (three lines at 375, one at 1280). The lede is six lines of slate at 375 (5.65:1, passes). The eyebrow repeats the title (L25).
- **Chooser cards**: The badges give a strong level identity and the "See level" links line up across columns at 1280. The cards are too tall at 375, the link target is small, and the content is repeated in the levels (L5).
- **"Pick one and start…" note**: Too wide at 1280 and placed between two things it doesn't belong to (L17, L24).
- **Level 1 card**: The cleanest card. Its success check and troubleshooting live outside it (L8).
- **Where do I paste it**: The most-referenced block, and the hardest to use (L10, L12).
- **Level 2 card**: Its bolded UI names and code chip set the right pattern for the page. The copy action is misplaced (L6), the prompt fold breaks at 375 (L1, L2), and the list indents are off (L21).
- **Level 3 card**: About 4,100px long at 375. It has three label styles (L8), unformatted literal strings and a dense step 5 (L9), alternatives in the wrong order (L19), a bold caption-like paragraph (L18), and a script fold that breaks at both widths (L1–L3).
- **Safety callout ("A script acts with your account")**: The bold lead-in bullets scan well. Nested inside the card, its text column at 375 is only about 237px (about 28 characters per line); at 375 it should run to the card's full width with 16px padding. It should be the only copper block (L12).
- **In a live session?**: Shown in the wrong state at the wrong size (L16, L13).
- **Level 4**: Its number and placement mislead (L15).
- **Footer**: The partner credit leads, and the author mark and license sit lower left, as fixed. "Site blocked at school?" is the escape hatch for locked-down district laptops, the audience most likely to hit Levels 2 and 3's failure cases, but it sits at the bottom of an 11,000px page. Consider linking to it from each level's "If it breaks".
- **The whole**: At 1280 the eye goes from the h1 to the three badges to the "See level" links, which is right. After that there is no primary action anywhere (L6), and the spacing doesn't separate levels from their sections (L11). The type scale holds for h1, h2, and the eyebrows, but sub-labels in Level 3 break it (L8). Copper is mostly disciplined but appears on two callouts (L12). The phone layout looks squeezed rather than designed: the fold rows collide, cards nest three deep, and a 263px column carries the core instructions. A first-time visitor would likely misread: the chooser cards as the whole content, Level 4 as a fourth level, "Copy for my AI" on Level 3 as the same as on Starters, and, during the event, "nothing to sign up for" as overriding the Planner.

## Keep
- The navy number badges as level identity, and the slate uppercase eyebrow as the section label (use it everywhere, L8).
- Level 2's patterns: bolded UI names, the `<!doctype html>` code chip, and an "If it breaks" list in every level.
- The safety callout's bold lead-in bullets and its dark-copper treatment, as the one emphasized block.
- Contrast holds wherever I measured: slate #45627D on cream at 5.65:1 and on card fill at 6.01:1, navy on Water tint at 10.1:1, button border against tint at about 3.3:1.
- 44px buttons, Montserrat on everything you press, and the shared left edge at 1280.
- The partner-first footer and the "Site blocked at school?" fallback.
