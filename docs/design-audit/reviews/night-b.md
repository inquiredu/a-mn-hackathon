# Night (dark theme): Grant a wish, Hosts, Sources · design review

Colors below were read from the screenshot pixels. The Night surfaces form four steps: chrome, bands, and input wells are #142434; the page is #1A2E41; cards are #20384F; raised blocks are #2E4B66. Reading text comes in two tones, cream #F4F1EB and a cool blue-gray #C8D2DA. Interactive borders are #9DAEBC. Phone shots are at 2x, so I give pixel figures in CSS px.

## The five that matter most

1. **E1 (Break).** On Hosts at 375px, the whole run of show runs off the right edge of the screen. Text, room tags, and probably each row's open/close control are cut off. The card's right edge never appears.
2. **E2 (Break).** On a phone, the nav row hides where you are. On Hosts and Sources the active tab is off-screen. On Grant a wish the active tab itself is cut to "Grant a w". Nothing shows that the row scrolls.
3. **E3 (Break).** The builder's "fill in all three" toast uses the same #2E4B66 fill as the wish preview it covers and has no border. It reads as a hole punched in the preview. It also still shows after all three answers are filled in.
4. **E4 (Break).** Placeholder text in the builder and in the Sources search is a neutral browser gray (#757575) on #142434, which is 3.42:1 and fails AA. It is the clearest sign of a theme that was inverted rather than designed. The unstyled gray checkboxes and radios (E19) are the second.
5. **E5 (Friction).** Night has no rule for which reading text is cream and which is gray. The same callout component is bright on one block and dim on the next. Lead paragraphs are dimmer than the bullet detail under them. The page flickers in brightness without meaning anything.

## Region by region

- **Header (all pages).** At 1280 it is clean and aligned to the 116/1164 content edges, with the active page in a cream pill and Present in an outlined pill. At 375 it has two rows and a scrolling tab row that hides the active page (E2). It isn't sticky, which hurts the long pages (E8). GitHub has no external cue (E32).
- **Wish hero.** The h1 is strong. The lead is an 11-line wall with an aside and a "Start here." that looks like a link but isn't (E10). It is also gray while later detail is cream (E5).
- **Wish builder (step 1).** The input wells (#142434 inside a #20384F card) read well. Placeholders fail AA (E4). The cream badge outshines the heading (E6). The preview's dashed border has no done state (E16). There is no field error state (E18). The toast is broken (E3). At 1280 the right side of the card is dead space (E16).
- **Wish steps 2–6.** Clear titles. Step 2 is dense. At 1280 the grid leaves an orphan cell (E24). Step 5's copy block uses a different layout from Hosts' copy blocks (E25).
- **Wish jar.** At 1280 the wrapped chips work. At 375 the list is long and ragged (E17), the wishes share the output surface (E7), and the next-step panel overlays the page (E15).
- **Wish callouts ("Where do I paste it", "Stuck?").** Copper is spent on help (E6). The two callouts sit at different brightness (E5). The inner cards are cramped at 375 (E31).
- **Wish "Keep it".** It uses a second numbering style (E25), shortcut chips that wrap mid-chord (E25), and merges into the footer (E26).
- **Hosts hero and "Two ways".** The eyebrow, h1, and lead are fine except for density and tone (E10, E5). The two cards are uneven at 1280 (E24).
- **Hosts "Who you need" / "Before the day".** Long bullets hide steps (E10). "To: your group" message block: the copy block itself works.
