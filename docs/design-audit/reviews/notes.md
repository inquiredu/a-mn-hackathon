# Speaker notes · design review

Measurements are in CSS px. The 560 and 375 shots are at 2x, so I halved image pixels; the 1280 shot is at 1x. Short names: **560-alone** = alone-screen01-560 (s01, s02), **375-alone** = alone-screen01-375 (s01, s02), **1280-alone** = alone-screen01-1280-s01, **s07** = with-stage-screen07-560 (s01, s02), **s10** = with-stage-screen10-560 (s01, s02), **timed-560** = with-stage-timed-560 (s01, s02), **timed-375** = with-stage-timed-375 (s01, s02). The timed captures show the stage on screen 9 ("Done one of these?", Show & Tell), seconds after the session was called to order, with the turn timer running.

Revision note: N1 is withdrawn; the screens it flagged were a captioning error in the packet, not a sync fault. N2 is rewritten against the timed captures. N3, N5, N6, N9, N11, N12, N14, N19, N21, the eye path and the Questions are revised. N22–N27 are new.

## The five that matter most
1. **N2 (Break).** The turn timer, the one thing the presenter must watch while a builder presents, is placed under the note, so where it lands depends on how long the note is. At the designed 560×780 window the readout "3:56" is cut in half by the fold (y≈750–780) and its controls are below it. At 375 the whole timer block is below the first screen. The presenter has stopped sharing at this point and is looking only at the notes.
2. **N6 (Break).** Once the clock has started, the window holds three reset-like controls: the timer's "Reset" beside "+1 min", "Call to order now" (still offered, and by its own help text it restarts the two-hour clock "from this moment"), and "Reset the session clock" right beside it. The note itself has to warn "(not Reset the session clock)". When the copy has to disambiguate the buttons, the buttons need to change.
3. **N3 (Break).** Back/Next fall below the fold at 560×780 on any note longer than about four lines. On timed-560 the timer block adds ≈160px, so Next sits roughly 300px below the fold, under the timer. The presenter scrolls to reach the timer, scrolls again to reach Next, and the window stays scrolled when the next screen loads (inferred).
4. **N24 (Break, verify).** The part line says "time to move on" seconds after the call to order, while the status row says "1:59:55 left". Two time signals in the same view disagree, and the stronger-sounding one is the wrong one: the presenter is ahead of schedule here, not behind.
5. **N7 (Friction).** Each note is one dense serif paragraph that mixes what to say, what to do, co-host actions, timer operation and advice for adapting the session. timed-560 is 8 lines (13 at 375) covering who to ask, how builders share, stopping your own share, the stage window's T, + and − keys, which Reset to use, and re-sharing the stage. A presenter who's talking can't scan it.

## Region by region
- **Intro paragraph (top, all captures):** written for the cold visitor, yet it stays during the live session, links three places that the next row links again, and on timed screens pushes the timer onto the fold. Keep it in the alone state only, under a real header (N4, N17).
- **Status row ("Screen n of 21" / "No session running" or "1:59:55 left"):** the clock readout works once called to order but has no label. Before the call to order it says "No session running", which misleads, and its weight is inverted. The connection status lives elsewhere. Unify into one labeled row (N5, N12, N15, N26).
- **Link row ("Open the site · Hosts · The stage"):** duplicates the intro, the labels don't match, and the stage link's target is unclear. One row, in the header, alone state only (N4, N13, N18).
- **Part band:** a good use of the Water tint. Its live pace status is set like the static facts, wraps with a leading "·", and on the timed screen contradicts the session clock (N11, N24).
- **"ON THE STAGE" eyebrow and title:** clear and correctly ranked; the balanced wrap on s07 works.
- **Note card:** rightly the largest text in the window and set in the reading face. It's flat: speech, actions, co-host jobs, timer operation and adaptation are run together (N7, N25). Surface detail (N21). On a phone, a smaller size (N19).
- **Timer card (timed screens):** the right controls (Pause, −1, +1, Reset), readable digits, and a clear eyebrow. Its placement makes it float with the note's length and fall on or below the fold (N2). It's the second navy-filled primary in view (N23). Its Reset is a third reset in the window (N6). The buttons wrap at 375 (N22). The digits are likely to jitter (N26), and its states are a word only (N27).
- **"Next:" line:** useful information in the wrong place; it belongs in the button (N8).
- **Back / Next:** one navy primary and a quiet secondary, at a good height (52px). They're positioned where the window's height can't guarantee they show (N3). Back has no disabled state at screen 1 (N16).
- **Connection hint ("Open the stage (present.html)…" / "Following the stage."):** this is the most important status, rendered as the least visible text, in a slot that makes it read as a caption for Back/Next (N5, N14).
- **Clock buttons:** unchanged after the call to order. "Call to order now" stays live and would restart the clock, and "Reset the session clock" sits beside it at equal weight (N6, N9).
- **Help paragraph:** button documentation and a keyboard legend run together in prose, describing the stage-window route even when the notes have buttons. Fold it into a `<details>` key legend (N9, N25).
- **Foot:** absent (N17, Questions).
- **1280:** the 560 layout stretched to 1240px; needs a measure and a rail (N10).
- **Eye path (timed-560):** the eye goes to the bold "1:59:55 left" at the top right, then the two-line tinted band ending in "time to move on", the title, eight lines of note, and finally a half-visible "3:56" on the fold. Pause and Next are off-screen. The order should be: the turn timer and its Pause, then what to say or do, then Next, with session time, screen and connection as one glance-line at the top. A docked timer (N2), a sticky action bar (N3) and the unified status row (N5) give that order. **Eye path (untimed screens, s07/s10):** the bold status, the band, the title, the note, then Next if it's on-screen. Note first, then time, then Next is the order to aim for.

## Keep
- One filled navy primary (Next) with an outlined Back, at a generous size, side by side even at 375.
- The note set as the largest text on the page, in Newsreader, with the copper rule marking it as the script.
- The "ON THE STAGE" eyebrow over the title, echoed by "TIMER ON THE STAGE": both say exactly what they show.
- The timer's control set: Pause, a minute off, a minute on, and a way back to the start, mirrored to the stage. Keep the controls; change the placement and treatment.
- The session readout as time left (not a clock time), consistent with the site's lengths-not-times rule.
- Part, length and room shown together for every screen.
- "Screen n of 21" always visible.
