# The Play window and the six starters · design review

Measurements are in CSS px. Phone shots are 2x, so a y of 690 in a phone image is 345 CSS px.

## The five that matter most
1. **Y1 (Break).** In the Play window at 375, the Meeting Timer runs past the frame's right edge: the Now card and the Agenda card lose their right borders, and the progress bar runs into the dialog edge. On its own at 375 it fits, so something in the timer needs about 340px, while the frame gives it 335. A narrow Google Site embed will clip it the same way.
2. **Y2 (Break).** The "Running your version." toast lands on top of Run my changes and Start over, the two buttons just pressed. At 1280 it also covers the agenda's minute figures. In light mode it's the same navy as the Run button, so the two merge into one shape.
3. **Y20 (Break).** The Oracle's Sound pill sits on top of the word "Lounge" in the title: in the Play window at 375 as soon as it opens, and in the standalone file at 375 once an answer appears.
4. **Y3 / Y4 (Friction).** The Play window header has no primary action. Copy for my AI, the reason the window exists, is styled the same as Show the code and Close. At 375 the three buttons wrap into an uneven 1+2 block (152px of a 772px dialog), and it reshuffles to 2+1 when "Show" becomes "Hide".
5. **Y14 (Friction).** The Group Maker's only filled button, Make groups, is at the bottom of the input card, below the fold at 960 and at 1280 in Play. It does the same job as the outlined Shuffle again at the top of the results. A visitor sees two weak buttons and misses the real one.

## Region by region
- **Backdrop and dialog shell**: the dialog fills almost the whole viewport, which suits trying a tool. On phones it's a card with 20px margins rather than a sheet, so page parts peek out and the frame loses 40px of width, which causes Y1 (Y8). In Night it needs the overlay shadow to stand off the navy page (Y8).
- **Header**: a cream strip with a hairline in light and navy in Night, matching the site. On desktop it has a good single line: title on the left, actions on the right. But it has no primary action (Y4), the title repeats the starter's H1 (Y9), and on phones it stacks into a 152px block that reflows when the toggle changes (Y3).
- **Frame**: the starter runs edge to edge under the header, with no inner border, which lets each starter's own look show, and that's right. The timer breaks out of it at 375 (Y1). Content cut off at the frame's bottom edge is an adequate scroll cue.
- **Code pane (desktop)**: a strong idea, well laid out: the preview on the left and the whole file on the right, with "PART 1: THE KNOBS" first, and Run my changes filled and next to the code. Fix the wrapping indentation (Y5) and the reset button's weight and label (Y7).
- **Code pane (phone)**: a 50/50 split leaves too little room for either half. Switch to tabs (Y5).
- **Toast**: it lands on the buttons (Y2) and reports in the wrong place (Y6).
- **Night theme**: the header, code pane and Run button invert well, and the cream-filled Run my changes in Night is a good choice. The starters stay light inside, so a bright white slab sits under the navy bar at 375 (see Questions).
- **Meeting Timer**: big digits and the "ends about" line work, and the agenda with its current-item highlight is clear. Clipped in Play at 375 (Y1). Control order and width jumps (Y11), dead space and the Chime jump (Y12), Chime state (Y13), agenda rows that look like buttons (Y25), and a footnote written for the frame (Y10).
- **Group Maker**: the group cards are clear and the orange headers have enough contrast (white on orange is about 5.4:1). The primary action is redundant and hidden (Y14), the phone layout is long (Y15), and the summary spacing is off (Y16).
- **Feedback Builder**: a good two-column setup at desktop, with live output and a clear green primary. The level scale wraps badly at mid widths (Y17), the output is off-screen on phones (Y18), and the output text needs structure (Y19).
- **Oracle**: the most crafted of the six, with real personality, and the two-column 960 layout is handsome. The Sound pill overlaps the title (Y20), the layout lurches on reveal and leaves an empty band before it (Y21), and the toggle has no state (Y13).
- **Charter Cards**: a clean accordion with a clear open state and +/− icons. The commitment pills look like buttons (Y22) and closed cards fade into the page (Y23).
- **Syllabus Page**: clear hierarchy, with the teal title, eyebrow section labels and month column. Its disclosures are invisible (Y24).
- **The set as a whole**: across all six the eye lands on the starter's H1 first, then its biggest control, which is right. The Play header comes third and should rank higher than it does for Copy for my AI (Y4). The starters share an unspoken grammar (title and subtitle, rounded white cards, pill buttons, a "made-up" footnote), but the same job is done differently from one to the next: disclosure has an icon in Charter and none in Syllabus; toggles show no state in Timer or Oracle; the primary sits above the results in Feedback but below the inputs in Group Maker. Since remixers learn patterns by copying these files, settle on one pattern per job: a plus/minus row for disclosure, an on/off label with `aria-pressed` for toggles, and one filled button placed right after what it acts on (Y13, Y14, Y24).

## Keep
- The desktop code view: the live preview beside the whole file, the knobs block first, and Run my changes as the one filled button in the pane.
- The cream header strip with a hairline over an edge-to-edge frame, so each starter keeps its own look.
- Run my changes inverting to a cream fill in Night.
- The timer's large digits and its "If you start now, ends about…" line.
- The Oracle's illustration, voice and its line saying nothing you type leaves the page.
- The Charter's accordion, with a 2px navy open state and +/− icons. Make it the pattern for Syllabus.
- The Feedback Builder's live-updating output and its Tone choice.
- Made-up data, labelled as made-up, in every starter.

## Questions for the owner
- After Run my changes, does Copy for my AI copy the edited version or the original? If it copies the edits, its label and its rank in the code view should say so. If not, people will lose their changes.
- Should the starters respect the viewer's dark preference (`prefers-color-scheme`) inside the Play window, or is a light starter under a Night header intended? If light is intended, a 1px cream-at-12% inset at the frame's top edge would soften the change.
- On phones, is the Play window the intended way to try a starter, or should it open full-screen in its own tab? The answer decides between the sheet and tabs (Y3, Y5, Y8) and a lighter phone path.
