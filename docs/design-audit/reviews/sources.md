# Sources · design review

Light theme, 375px and 1280px, default ("after"), during (now bar), a start-here path chosen, a topic filter on, the filters panel open, and a search with no results. Measurements are in CSS px. Phone shots were halved from 2x.

## The five that matter most

1. **R2 (Break):** On a laptop the sticky filter column is taller than the window. "Kind of source" is cut off after "Essays and commentary", so two kinds (4 of the 45 sources) can't be seen, and nothing hints that the column scrolls. At 1280×720, the usual Chromebook and district-laptop size, more is cut.
2. **R1 (Break):** The phone header cuts its nav mid-word ("Grant a v") and hides 4 of 8 destinations, including **Sources**. On this page you can't see where you are, and nothing suggests the strip scrolls.
3. **R4 + R3 (Friction):** A source card uses five chip-like styles for three different jobs: a disclosure, filter buttons, and status labels. Nothing tells you that tapping a topic tag filters all 45 sources. When a card has two or three tags they stack into a narrow column, and "What it says" floats beside it, centred and out of line. That happens on about a third of the cards at both widths.
4. **R6 + R5 (Friction):** On a phone the first source card starts about 3,190px down (4.6 screens), and the page is 19,861px long (about 28 screens). The six path cards alone take about 1,270px. "Copy a link to this entry" gets its own row on every card, which adds about 1,800px.
5. **R10 + R14 (Friction):** You can't see what state the page is in. A chosen path gets only a copper border, the same look as the focus ring. A `?path=` link opens on a first screen that hasn't changed. "Showing 5 of 45 sources." never says which filter is on, even though there are five ways to narrow the list.

## Region by region

- **Header (375):** Fails to show where you are (R1). The 92px two-row header takes 13% of the first screen, and a menu button would fit in one 56px row.
- **Header (1280):** Clean. The active "Sources" cream pill is clear, and "Present" as the one outlined pill stands apart. "GitHub" leaves the site with no "↗" (polish, same idea as R18).
- **Now bar (during):** Clear at 1280. Wraps badly at 375 (R24). The copper "NOW" pill is the right single accent.
- **Hero:** The eyebrow repeats the h1 (R23). The lede is a six-job paragraph in the lightest text colour (R7). There's no primary action in the first screen at either width. The Start-here cards should be that action.
- **The short version:** The right idea, set as a 17-line block (R8). The one tinted box should be the easiest thing to skim, not the hardest.
- **Start here:** Six good entry questions, but the cards don't look pressable (R9). They take 1,270px on a phone (R6). They orphan two slots at 1280 (R11). The selected state is a copper border that looks like the focus ring, and nothing points to the result (R10).
- **Browse tabs:** Fine at 1280. At 375 they become a ragged stack of four buttons (R12). Counts update under filters, good, but tabs with 0 still look live (R15).
- **Filters (phone):** A lone button in a card, with no open state and no "show results" action (R13).
- **Filters (desktop):** A good two-facet column with counts, but it outgrows the window (R2), uses serif labels for controls, and cuts off its placeholder (R22).
- **Count line:** "All 45 sources." / "Showing 5 of 45 sources." is the right anchor. It needs the active filters named (R14), and "Open every summary" should hide at 0 (R15).
- **Section headings and group eyebrows:** The hierarchy is too flat for a 45-card list (R16). The group names themselves are good.
- **Source card:** The content model is excellent: title, provenance, kind, one-sentence "For the session" or "The habit". The controls under it are the weak part: five chip styles (R4), a tag-wrap bug (R3), an oversized copy button (R5), a disclosure with no state (R26), a wandering badge (R19), random tag order (R20), heavy copper underlines (R18), and a long measure at desktop (R21).
- **Filtered state:** Works, and the navy active tag inside each card echoes the filter nicely. But the filter itself isn't named at the top (R14).
- **Empty state:** Exists and offers a way out. It duplicates Clear, removes the controls you'd adjust, and doesn't repeat the query (R15).
- **How the session is built on this:** The best "why" on the page, placed last and set as a wall of text, with buttons that don't say what they do (R17).
- **Footer:** The partner credit and author sign-off are correct and untouched. The check date is repeated with drift (R25). "Site blocked at school?" is a valuable fallback, rightly kept.
- **The whole (375):** The eye goes header, h1, a grey paragraph, a teal paragraph, then four screens of path cards, then a hidden filter, then 45 cards. The visitor's real first move (pick a path or find a source) comes too late, and the page gives no way back up. Fix order: R6, R7, R8, R13.
- **The whole (1280):** It reads much better. The two-column browse is the page's strongest layout. The eye lands on the h1 and lede, then the short version, then a path grid with holes. Copper appears on every title, so it doesn't mark any single thing. Spacing between sections, groups, and cards is too even to show the structure (R16).

## Keep

- The card's content model: title, then author · publisher · date, then kind, then a one-sentence note that says what the source means *for you* ("For the session", "The habit").
- "Proceed with care" pairing each caution with a habit.
- The provenance labels: Research, Peer-reviewed, Preprint, Official help. Keep the distinction, even if the styling changes (R4).
- Counts everywhere: on tabs, on filter options, and "Showing N of 45".
- The navy active tag inside a card when that topic is the filter.
- The desktop two-column browse with a sticky filter column, once R2 is fixed.
- An empty state that offers a way out.
- Balanced wrapping on long titles.
- The footer's "Site blocked at school?" fallback.

## Questions for the owner

- What should pressing a Start-here path do: filter the list to those sources in reading order, scroll to them, or both? Should other filters clear when a path is chosen?
- Do you want topic tags on a card to filter the whole page? If they're meant only as labels, they should stop looking like buttons. If they're filters, R4's pill treatment applies.
- Who uses "Copy a link to this entry", and how often? If it's mainly hosts preparing chat messages, could it move inside the opened summary?
- What do the two buttons under "How the session is built on this" do today?
- On a phone, is the full 45-card "Everything" list the default you want, or would you accept sections showing 3 cards each with "Show all 20"?
