# The stage · design review

Reviewed: all 21 screens at 1280×720 and 375px, every session-clock state, the screen timer (ready on screen-09, running and paused on the replacement shots taken on screen 9), the open clock with and without a wait, and the next-screen motion strip. Measurements are in screenshot pixels at 1280 and CSS px at 375 (phone shot pixels ÷ 2). Contrast figures come from sampling the PNGs.

## The five that matter most
1. **P1 · The title screen's clock lands on top of text.** Before Call to order, the copper "2:00:00" sits on the small-print paragraph. Just after, it sits on the second line of the subtitle. Screen 1 is the first thing the room sees, so the first frame shows a collision.
2. **P2 · On a phone the stage clips and covers its own content, and it can't scroll.** The URL plate (s4), the third award (s16) and the timer buttons (s20) disappear under the rail or the floating clock pill. The Oracle embed (s3) is cut off.
3. **P3 · The rule for presenter-only controls is inconsistent.** The top-right controls are ghosted at 25% (text about 2.2:1, an AA failure for the presenter), still show to the room as faint boxes, and sit over the s4 heading. The other presenter-only controls (Call to order, Pause/Reset, timer buttons, wait buttons) show at full strength on the shared slide.
4. **P4 · Screens have no fixed top line.** Every screen's content is centred vertically, so the heading's top edge falls anywhere from 30px to 270px, and it jumps on every instant cut. The tall screens (s3, s4) get pushed up into the control band.
5. **P8 · Much of the text won't survive a Meet tile.** At the half size a participant usually sees, Montserrat Light at 21–26px (ladder and award descriptions, the s3 caption) shrinks to roughly 11px of thin stroke. The rail label drops to about 9.5px. The title screen's small print, about 10px to begin with, becomes unreadable.

## Region by region
- **Top controls (1280):** six buttons, ← and → split about 600px apart, "Back to the site" (which leaves the stage) wedged between Speaker notes and Full screen. Ghosted at 25% (P3). Group them as [← →] · Jump to now · Speaker notes · Full screen, with Back to the site set 24px apart at the left of the group.
- **Key-hint pill:** P10.
- **Title screen (s1):** the partner credit row is well set. Title and subtitle are strong; the clock collision is P1, the small print P8, the inset P29.
- **Prompt screens (s2, s5, s6, s8, s9, s12–15):** the clearest pattern in the deck: one ask, one copper line, one sentence. Hierarchy is P12, anchoring P4, copper on s8 P11.
- **Embed (s3):** P4 (heading crushed under the controls, caption stranded), P2 on phone.
- **Plan (s4):** a clean table; P3 collision, P11 copper, P19 phone, P2 plate overflow on phone.
- **Ladder (s7, s18):** good callback between the two; type is P8/P9, row 4 is P26.
- **Steps (s10, s11):** P9 face, P24 alignment.
- **Screen timer (s8, s9, s13–15, s20):** a good eyebrow pattern (READY / RUNNING / PAUSED · n MIN), true minus signs, tabular digits that don't jitter. Problems: P5, P6, P7, P14, and the buttons in P3.
- **Awards (s16):** P25; descriptions too light for Meet (P8); clipped on phone (P2).
- **Code (s17):** P27.
- **Quote (s19):** right as it is; at 375 the quote mark sits 8px under the controls (resolved by P18).
- **Pair (s20):** the densest screen: P11, and P2 on phone.
- **Closing (s21):** P20.
- **Rail:** part · length · room is useful orientation. P23 cluster, P8 label size, P17/P18 phone.
- **Session clock:** hero P1, corner pill P13, open pane P15/P21, motion P22. The over state is good (see Keep).

## Keep
- Navy stage, cream type, one copper accent on most screens, one idea per screen.
- Instant cuts between screens: right for Meet, where fades turn into encoder mush.
- The over-time state: "+0:10:01 over" in copper with the rail's slate bar filled. Quiet, no red, no alarm.
- The timer eyebrow pattern and the −1 min / +1 min adjustments; tabular digits.
- The URL plate as a single, unmistakable address.
- The wait pill that follows the clock to the corner.
- The ladder repeated on s7 and s18 as a callback.

## Questions for the owner
- s13 and s14: is 57:00 one breakout timer carried across screens, or two separate timers? (P5)
- Should the room see Call to order and the timer buttons (as theatre), or should they be presenter-only like the top controls? (P3)
- Is the title screen's small print meant for the gathering room, or only for visitors who find the stage later? (P8)
- Does the screen timer have a last-minute or time-up state? None was shown. (P6)
- On a phone, is the co-host meant to drive the stage or only follow along? (P18)
