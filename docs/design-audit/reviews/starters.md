# Starters · design review

Scope: the Starters page at 375px and 1280px in light, the Remix menu, the next-step panel, the copy toast and the copy-by-hand dialog. For the Play window I judged only the hand-off from the page. Pixel figures are CSS px, worked out from the screenshots (phone shots ÷2), so treat them as ±5px.

## The five that matter most
1. **S1 · The starters are buried.** At 375px the first starter card begins about 3,015px down the page, nearly four phone screens. Above it sit a 7-line lede, a heavy audience box (≈300px), four step cards (≈860px) and an always-open paste guide (≈1,300px). At 1280px the first card starts at ≈1,360px. The page's subject, and its one primary action (Play), never shows in the first view on either width.
2. **S2 · Opening a Remix menu at 1280px breaks the Level 1 row.** The three neighbouring cards stretch to the open card's height, so their Play, Copy and Remix controls drop out of view. The remix lines get squeezed into a ≈123px text column, 5 to 7 lines per line of copy.
3. **S3 · Script cards make you scroll ≈1,100–1,440px between "1. Press Copy the script" and the Copy the script button.** The steps are also walls of text: step 5 runs 17 lines. Values you have to find exactly (`FOLDER_ID`, the Drive URL) are set in the serif body face and wrap mid-token.
4. **S4 · Level 2 runs five numbered steps inline in one ≈480px paragraph, and Level 3 explains its two buttons in a 17-line paragraph.** Each script card then repeats the "script, not a page" summary in bold. These need lists and labelled rows, not prose.
5. **S5 · Copy feedback misses the point of action.**
   - The pressed button never changes.
   - At 375px the toast is a 186px-wide, 3-line box over the remix lines. In the Play window the same toast covers the button that was just pressed.
   - The next-step panel floats over the card with no backdrop, and its Open Claude button is 10px shorter than its siblings.
   - The copy-by-hand dialog is half empty, and its instructions undo the selection it has already made.

## Region by region
- **Header (375, 1280)**: The navy bar and the cream active pill are clear. At 375 the nav is clipped, and four destinations, Present among them, are hidden (S21). At 1280 the outlined "Present" pill sits among nav links and reads like a nav item with a border. ⚠️ Whether visitors should see it is the owner's call.
- **Now bar (during)**: The copper NOW pill is a good single accent. The self-link and the stray separator are S20.
- **Hero (eyebrow, H1, lede)**: The type is right: tracked slate eyebrow, big Montserrat H1. The lede is 7 lines at 375 and does a navigation job in prose ("four… two… two…", "the three levels"). Turn that sentence into the level index row (S1).
- **Audience box**: Over-emphasised, and it puts three audiences in one paragraph (S8).
- **How-to steps 1–4**: Four boxes for four sentences, with badges that look like buttons (S7).
- **"Where do I paste it"**: Card-in-card with a flat hierarchy and mixed success and failure lines. It duplicates the next-step panel and should fold (S12, S1).
- **Level headings and intros**: Section breaks are weak, the levels are shaped differently, and Levels 2 and 3 carry prose walls (S18, S4, S5).
- **Starter card (Levels 1–2)**: The order is right: who it's for, name, what it does, Monday use, then Play and Copy, then Remix. Problems are the tile, the bar and the hue (S14, S15), the caps eyebrows (S15), title misalignment and voids at 1280 (S13), and the Remix summary's weight (S16).
- **Remix menu**: The three groups ("Make it yours / useful / fun") are a good structure. Rows, blanks and naming are S16. At 1280 it breaks the grid (S2).
- **"What Google Sites does to pasted code"**: Fine as a disclosure, but it uses the paste guide's static container (S19).
- **Script cards (Level 3)**: The content model (Before you start, Steps, You'll know it worked, If it breaks) is strong. The buttons are in the wrong place (S3), the bold summary repeats the intro (S5), code isn't in mono and step 5 is a wall (S6), and the buttons stack unevenly (S23). At 1280 they should run one per row (S13).
- **Next-step panel**: The right content at the right moment, in the wrong container (S10).
- **Toast**: Too narrow on a phone, covers content, and is used in place of feedback on the button itself (S9).
- **Copy-by-hand dialog**: Half empty, with instructions that don't fit the device and no route back to the AI (S11).
- **Play hand-off**: The "Play" label and a dialog title that matches the card make a clean hand-off, and "Copy for my AI" uses the same label inside and outside the dialog, which is good. At 375 the dialog's header controls wrap to two rows (Play reviewer's area). The shared toast covering "Run my changes" is in S9.
- **Footer**: The credits are fixed and fine. "Safe play" restates the Group Maker note: keep it, and with S22 the in-card note becomes the one people actually see.

## Keep
- Eight real tools, each with a "Monday:" line that names a use. That line is the page's best idea.
- Play, Copy for my AI and Remix as the three verbs on every card, in that order, with Play as the only filled button.
- The Remix menu's three groups and its fill-in-the-blank remix lines.
- The script cards' structure: Before you start, Steps, You'll know it worked, If it breaks.
- A next-step panel that names the three AIs, each with its one-line hint, right after copying.
- A copy-by-hand fallback that preselects the text.
- Cream, navy and copper used with restraint in the hero; consistent 116px left alignment at 1280.

## Questions for the owner
- In the copy-by-hand dialog the text ends "Please run it as a live preview…" in both shots. Is that a placeholder from the screenshot run, or does the box really hold only a preview? If it's real, it's a Break: copying by hand would paste incomplete code.
- Are the per-card hues meant to echo each tool's own colours? If so, should the page say so, or should the hue stay inside the icon only (S14)?
- Should "Present" appear in the public header for every visitor, on phones too?
- During a live session, should this page reorder for participants: live-session guidance first, paste guide open (S8, S1)?
