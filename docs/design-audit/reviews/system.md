# The design system across the site · design review

Sources: the 28 images in the packet (top two screens and foot of each page at 1280 light; each page's header at 1280 and 375 light) and facts/inventory.md (measured looks, states, type styles, measure, backgrounds, radii). Anything taken only from the inventory, and not visible in a screenshot, says "(inventory)". Anything I'm guessing at says "(inferred)".

Colors are named by role: navy #20384F, navy-900 #142434, slate text #45627D, slate border #6E8599, hairline #DCD5C7, card #FAF8F4, sand band #EAE5DB, Water tint #E3EDF0, copper tint #F7EBE0.

## The five that matter most

1. **X1 (Break). The phone header breaks on every page.** At 375px the nav is one row wider than the screen. "Grant a w" is cut off mid-word. On Hosts and Sources the highlighted page is off-screen, so a visitor can't tell where they are, and GitHub and Present can't be seen at all. The inventory measures each page at 769–851px wide inside a 375px viewport, so the whole page scrolls sideways.
2. **X3 (Friction). Shape doesn't tell you what you can press.** The number badges are navy rectangles with 4px corners, the same as the primary button. The step cards on Home and Levels, which you can't press, look exactly like the "Everything here" cards, which you can. The path cards on Sources are buttons drawn as a third kind of card. The selected tab on Sources looks like a primary call to action.
3. **X12, with X2 (Friction; X2 is a Break). The site barely responds when you touch it.** No element has a pressed state. Inline links, disclosures, the outline buttons in the Home hero, and the stage controls don't change on hover. Buttons have no transitions, so their hover color snaps. Clickable cards don't lift. Present's hover appears to fill the pill cream behind cream text, which would make the label vanish (X2, inferred).
4. **X4 (Friction). The type scale has no levels, only sizes.** Each page uses 30–38 distinct type styles, from about 22 Montserrat sizes and 7 Newsreader sizes, several of them 0.2–0.6px apart (16.8/17/17.6, 14.4/14.72/15.2). Card titles appear six ways. An h2 can be 30px or 20px. An h3, h4, or h5 can be a real heading or a 12px uppercase eyebrow. A heading's level doesn't predict how it looks.
5. **X9 (Friction). Boxes multiply.** I count about ten panel and callout treatments. Where you stand ("On your own? / In a live session?") is boxed three different ways on three pages. The Water tint does seven jobs. A dashed border means both "copy this text" and "later". On Levels, Starters, and Wish, bordered cards sit inside a copper-barred panel. A visitor can't learn what any box means.

## Region by region

- **Header (desktop)**: Its job is to say where you are and let you go anywhere. The navy bar and cream current-page pill do that well at 1280. Problems: weight jitter on the current item (X21), a wordmark no bigger than the nav (X21), Present outshouting the pages (X20), GitHub unmarked as external (X20), and a hover on Present that may hide its label (X2).
- **Header (phone)**: Broken (X1): a clipped, sideways-scrolling row that hides the current page and both host tools, and pushes the page to 769–851px wide.
- **Page header (eyebrow, H1, lede)**: Mostly one pattern, but Wish lacks the eyebrow (X17). Home's eyebrow is tracked differently (X17). The H1 sits closer to its lede than an H2 does to its intro (X16). Ledes are slate and quieter than body text (X7), and run 3–8 lines (X8).
- **Section headers**: H2 at 30px is consistent where used, but the same tag also appears at 20px, and 12px uppercase kickers stand in for real headings inside cards (X4, X5). "Start here" on Sources is crammed against its intro (X16).
- **Body text**: Newsreader 17/1.6 navy is comfortable at 672px. In wide cards it runs to 100–143 characters per line (X6). Slate carries too much primary text (X7).
- **Cards**: The base card (#FAF8F4, hairline, 8px) is good. Static and clickable cards look the same, and path cards are a third look (X3). Card titles appear six ways (X4). Card padding takes eight values (X16). Starter cards misalign their titles and add four hues (X19). Grids leave orphan rows (X18).
- **Panels and callouts**: About ten treatments with overlapping meanings (X9), copper used as a panel bar (X10), and a panel whose title matches its children (X26).
- **Buttons**: The primary is solid, and 44px is the right default. The secondary has two looks, and heights drift (X11). There are no pressed states, hover often snaps, and copy confirmation sits far from the button (X12). Number badges imitate the primary (X3).
- **Links**: Five looks (X15). Copper underlines at rest spend the brand's one accent (X10). No hover (X12).
- **Disclosures**: Four looks with default markers (X13). Hosts places a visible paragraph under a closed one (X27).
- **Form controls**: Three inputs, unstyled 18px checkboxes, likely low-contrast placeholders (X14).
- **Badges, tags, kbd**: Badges read as buttons (X3). Topic tags are rectangles rather than the brand's pills (X23). kbd appears in two looks (X23). Two numbering series collide on Wish (X24).
- **Section bands and spacing**: Alternating cream and sand bands are a quiet, good divider. Section padding drifts from 57 to 96px, and dividers come in four kinds (X16).
- **Footer**: The structure is right (partner leads, author signs lower left). Sources drops the safety line, and the partner tagline leaves an orphan word (X22).
