# Grant a wish · design review

Light theme, 375px and 1280px, after and during, plus builder states (empty copy, filled), the jar's next-step panel, and the header. Measurements: phone values are CSS px (screenshot px ÷ 2). Colors and contrast ratios were sampled from the screenshot pixels.

## The five that matter most
1. **The builder's empty and error states mislead (W6, W7).** With nothing filled in, the preview shows the template's bracket tokens in the same navy serif as a real wish, under a label that says "ready to paste", next to a button that looks ready to press. The only error is a toast that covers the preview. In both "filled" captures the same toast still says "Fill in all three answers first" while all three answers are filled in.
2. **Two contrast failures under AA (W9, W30).** Placeholder text (#757575 on #FAF8F4) measures about 4.3:1, below 4.5:1, and it carries the example answers. The copper focus ring as specified (#C47D4D) measures 2.92:1 on the cream page and 2.62:1 on the "Keep it" band, below the 3:1 needed for non-text contrast. Using the dark copper token for the ring on light surfaces fixes it.
3. **The phone header cuts off the page you're on (W1).** At 375px the active pill reads "Grant a w", and Hosts, Sources, GitHub and Present are off-screen with no sign the row scrolls.
4. **The jar is far from the builder it replaces (W14, W15).** The jar is the other way to do step 1, but on a phone its heading starts about 1,330px below "Copy my wish", past five step cards. Pressing a jar wish also skips the builder: it copies text the visitor never sees and can't edit. Move the jar next to the builder, and have a press fill the three answers.
5. **The same instructions for each AI appear four times, in two different orders (W13).** They're in the hero, step 2, "Where do I paste it", and the jar panel. The sharing and screenshot sections repeat the pattern with a card per tool and per computer. One "Which AI are you using?" choice, remembered on the device, plus a computer choice set from the device, would cut roughly 1,000px from a 7,823px phone page and leave one card per question.

## Region by region
- **Header (W1, W2)**: At 1280 it's clean and right-aligned, and the active pill reads at a glance. At 375 the nav cuts off the current page and hides four destinations. It isn't sticky, which is right for a long reading page, but a long page then needs a way back up (consider a "Back to the builder" anchor at the end of each section).
- **Now bar (W3)**: The copper-tint NOW pill and quiet cream text work. On this page it should confirm "You're here", not link to itself.
- **Hero (W4)**: The H1 is right-sized and on the 116px grid. The intro is the region most in need of structure. It delays the builder and buries an unlinked instruction.
- **Step 1, the builder (W5, W6, W7, W8, W9, W12)**: This is the page's reason to exist, and the core works: live preview, clear labels, one navy primary button. Its weak points are the states (empty, error, the stale toast), the field shapes (the third field clips), placeholder contrast, and an unused right half at desktop.
- **Steps 2–6 (W10, W11, W13)**: Good, short headings ("Make it yours", "Break it, fix it"). As boxes they're heavy on a phone and full of holes at desktop. Step 2 holds a long chain of per-tool instructions that belongs in one tool-aware component.
- **Wish jar (W14, W15, W16, W17)**: A delightful idea in the wrong place, styled like text to copy, with no record of what you took.
- **Next-step panel (W18, W19, W20)**: The right moment to help ("now paste it"). The panel neither belongs to the page nor behaves as a dialog, and its buttons look like inputs.
- **Where do I paste it (W13, W21, W24, W25)**: Exact and useful content in a card-in-card-in-callout layout with smaller type and very long bullet lines. It becomes the tabbed paste component.
- **Stuck? Three moves. (W21, W22)**: Bold leads ("Describe it.", "Rewind.", "Ask for help.") scan well. The third bullet needs splitting and a copy box.
- **Keep it, and show one person (W10, W26, W27, W28)**: The tonal band clearly marks a new chapter. The keycaps are excellent. The numbering clashes with the page's steps, the live-session part should fold on ordinary days, and the privacy caution needs to be seen.
- **Footer**: Credits as fixed. "Safe play" and "Site blocked at school?" are useful on this page. Only the link weight differs from the body (W29).
