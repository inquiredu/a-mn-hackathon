Take me there

For hosts, co-hosts, and anchors

Running the morning

Two hours, about an hour of it building in breakout rooms of four. The participant pages, the stage, and this guide all read from one session file, so what you see here is what everyone else sees.

Materials

Present the stage Speaker notes The participant page

  • 2hours, 10:00 to noon Central
  • 60minutes of building in breakout rooms
  • 4people at most in each room, one anchor each
  • 6parts, each with a stage, a checklist, and messages

Forming teams of four

  1. 10:00 to 10:05, main room. Anyone who has built something interactive with AI, even once, adds their name to the first table in the Teams doc. They're the anchors.
  2. 10:08 to 10:18, while the live build runs. The co-host counts the builders, opens Breakout rooms with the number below, presses Shuffle, then drags one anchor into each room and fills in the Room column for the anchors.
  3. 10:20. Open the rooms. Everyone adds their name to their room's row in the Teams doc. That's how we know the teams.
  4. Latecomers. Add them to the room with the fewest people, and add their name to that room's row.

Run of show

Each part lists what's on the stage (with links that open the presenter view at that screen), a checklist for hosts, and messages ready to paste. Checklist ticks are saved in this browser only.

Before, during, and after

Before Friday

  • Make the gallery slide and the wonder and worry slide, and add their links to the session file.
  • Share the Teams doc so anyone with the link can edit. Friday morning is a good time.
  • Paste a starter into Gemini on a district account (choose Canvas first), and check that it runs. ChatGPT now previews code with a Preview switch instead of Canvas.
  • Name the co-host who will set up the breakout rooms.
  • Rehearse with the stage in one window and the speaker notes in another.

In the rooms

  • The anchor shares their screen for the first five minutes. Everyone builds along, then builds their own.
  • Breakout chats start empty: paste the page link when you visit a room.
  • People press Ask for help in Meet to call a host.
  • At 11:10: ten minutes left, pin something to the wall, even if it's half-built.

Safe play

  • Made-up names and made-up data only. Nothing about real students goes into an AI.
  • Names typed into a running tool stay on that computer. Names pasted into an AI chat go to that company.
  • Public events only in the Calendar Explorer.
  • Before anyone shares a tool beyond their team, check it with a keyboard, for contrast, and for labels.

Show & Cheer and after

  • Builders share with Present now, then A tab, so their tab's sound comes through.
  • If sharing fails, show their screenshot from the gallery wall.
  • People keep their own work. We don't collect it, and the wall stays inside the group.
  • The follow-up points to this site and to next month's session on assessment.
