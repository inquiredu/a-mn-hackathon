The Teacher's Lounge Oracle

Ask a question. Get a dramatic, slightly ridiculous prophecy, with a gong.

Remix menu
Make it silly
  • Make the Oracle talk like an over-excited game-show host.
  • Add a drumroll before the gong, and confetti when the prophecy appears.
  • Write 10 new prophecies about my school, ___. Keep them kind.
Make it yours
  • Change the colors to my school colors: ___ and ___.
  • Add a button that reads the prophecy out loud.
  • Add a button that switches everything to Spanish.
  • Teach the Oracle a new topic: when I ask about ___, it should answer with prophecies about ___.
Make it useful
  • Turn it into a vocabulary oracle. Each spin reveals one of these words and what it means: ___.
  • Turn it into a discussion-question picker for my class on ___.
  • Turn it into a name picker for my class. I'll type in made-up names.Why made-up? Whatever you paste into an AI tool goes to that company. Real student names stay out.
Look inside (no AI needed)
  • Press Play, then Show the code. Find SPIN_SECONDS, change the 3 to a 10, and press Run my changes.
  • In the code, find the coffee topic under TOPICS. Add your own prophecy in quotes, end it with a comma, press Run my changes, and ask about coffee.
  • Break it on purpose: delete one } and press Run my changes. Then copy the broken code to your AI and ask, "I broke it. Can you find and fix it?"

Where do I paste it?

Gemini

Open gemini.google.com. Turn on Canvas in the prompt bar (it may be under Tools), then paste.

Claude

Open claude.ai and paste. The machine appears beside the chat as an artifact.

ChatGPT

Open chatgpt.com, choose Canvas from the tools, paste, then press Preview.

Grant a wish

Now build something from nothing. It's the same loop you just used, minus the training wheels. It starts with you, the AI does the heavy lifting, and it comes back to you.

  1. 1

    Say what you wish for

    One or two sentences. Who is it for, and what should it do? Fill in the blanks:

    Build me a small web app: [what it is] for [who it's for]. It should [what it does]. Make it simple, colorful, and fun to use. Use made-up data.

  2. 2

    Hand it to your AI

    Paste it into Gemini with Canvas on, Claude, or ChatGPT with Canvas. A first version usually shows up in under a minute.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    Ask for one change at a time. "Make the buttons bigger." "Add a sound when I win." "Use our school colors."

  4. 4

    Break it, fix it

    Something will go sideways. Tell the AI exactly what you see: "When I press Start, nothing happens."

  5. 5

    Peek under the hood

    Explain three lines of this code to me like I've never coded before. Pick the three most interesting ones.

  6. 6

    Back to you

    Is it what you wished for? Who would you show it to? Pin it to the wall.

Stuck? Three moves.

  • Describe it. Tell the AI exactly what you see and what you expected instead.
  • Rewind. "Go back to the version before that one."
  • Say so. Post in the chat. Someone has been exactly where you are.
