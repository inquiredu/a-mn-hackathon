The Group Maker

For teachers, coaches, and anyone leading a workshop

Paste names, pick a size, and shuffle. It spreads your helpers out, one per group. Keeping two people apart is one remix away.

Monday: tomorrow's lab groups, made in a second.

Real names stay out of the AI. Names typed into the tool here stay in this browser window; nothing is saved or sent. Once the tool runs inside your AI chat, it runs on that company's page, so keep to made-up names there.

Remix menu
Make it yours

  • Make the group cards big enough to read on a projector from the back of the room.
Make it useful
  • Add a box where I can type pairs who should be kept apart.
  • Give each group roles: facilitator, recorder, timekeeper, and reporter.
  • Let me save a list of names in this browser so I don't paste it every time.
Make it fun
  • Make the shuffle dramatic: names spin and land one at a time, with a drumroll.
Look inside (no AI needed)

Level 2

On your site

Pages, not tools: each one ends up on a Google Site you can edit. Play it, hand it to your AI with your own document, and paste what comes back into your Site.

How to put it on your Site, in five steps
  1. Press Copy for my AI and paste into your AI chat (where, above).
  2. Copy a remix line such as "Replace the charter with this document," paste it, and paste your document's text where the ___ is.
  3. When it looks right, copy the finished code out (each tool's spot is in Where to paste it).
  4. In your Site: Insert, Embed, the Embed code tab, paste, Next, Insert.
  5. Drag the box until the whole page shows. Before you publish, read the page against your document; the AI keeps the meaning, and you check every line.

No Site yet? sites.google.com, then Blank site. You can also put a starter on a Site as it is: press Play, then Show the code, select it all, copy, and paste it into the Embed code tab. The full steps, and what to do when Sites refuses it: Three levels, level 2.

What Google Sites does to pasted code

Google Sites shows it inside a frame on your page. That keeps your site safe, and it sets the limits: the page can't remember anything between visits, so saved settings and scores vanish; it can't reach the rest of your site or your visitors' accounts; sound and full screen wait for a tap; and a very long page may be refused or load slowly. It shows only as big as you drag the frame. Cut off, or misbehaving? Copy the request below into your AI.

If what you pasted doesn't fit or doesn't behave, hand your AI this:

Make this one self-contained HTML file for a Google Sites embed box about 800 pixels wide: no outside libraries, fonts, or images; nothing saved between visits; no sound until a tap; large type that works on a phone; and as short as it can be while keeping everything it does.

Level 3

Behind the scenes

Scripts, not pages: nothing to play here. A script is a few instructions Google runs for you inside your Drive, in a tool called Apps Script that's built into every Google account; you approve what it may touch before it runs.

Copy the script
The script alone, for script.google.com.
Copy for my AI
The script with a note asking your AI to explain it line by line before you run it. A remix line or an error message only makes sense to your AI once it has the script, so paste this first. When your AI hands back a changed script, paste the whole thing over the old one in your project, save, and run again.

Each card has the whole path: what to make first, every step with its place, what you'll see when it worked, and what to do when it breaks. The cautions, on Three levels.

The Drive Inventory

For curriculum leads and anyone with a deep folder

Point it at a folder. It reads every folder inside and writes a Sheet: each document, its type, its link, and when it last changed.

Monday: a whole curriculum folder, one search away.

Apps Script · your Drive · about an hour the first time, fifteen minutes after

Remix menu
Make it useful
  • Add a column for who owns each file.
  • Skip anything that hasn't changed in two years, and list those in a second tab instead.
  • Make the Link column a clickable "Open" instead of the whole address.

Remix menu
