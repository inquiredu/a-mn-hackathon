The Meeting Timer

For anyone who runs a meeting

Your agenda on screen with a big countdown, a gentle chime when it's time to move on, and a parking lot.

Monday: run your next PLC with it on the screen.

Remix menu
Make it yours
  • Change the agenda to my meeting: ___ (10 minutes), ___ (15 minutes), ___ (5 minutes).
  • Show our team norms along the bottom: ___.
  • Use our school colors: ___ and ___.
Make it useful
  • Add a protocol mode: 5 minutes of silent writing, then 10 minutes of discussion, with a different chime for each.
  • Add a spinner that picks who takes notes and who keeps time.
  • Add a button that copies the parking lot as a list I can paste into our notes.
Make it fun
  • When time runs out, play a game-show buzzer instead of the chime.
  • If we finish the agenda early, celebrate with confetti.
Look inside (no AI needed)
  • Press Play, then Show the code. Find AGENDA, change the minutes for one item, and press Run my changes.

The Group Maker

For teachers, coaches, and anyone leading a workshop

Paste names, pick a size, and shuffle. It spreads your helpers out, one per group. Keeping two people apart is one remix away.

Monday: tomorrow's lab groups, made in a second.

Real names stay out of the AI. Names typed into the tool here stay in this browser window; nothing is saved or sent. Once the tool runs inside your AI chat, it runs on that company's page, so keep to made-up names there.

Remix menu
Make it yours
  • Use these group names instead: ___.
  • Make the group cards big enough to read on a projector from the back of the room.
Make it useful
  • Add a box where I can type pairs who should be kept apart.
  • Give each group roles: facilitator, recorder, timekeeper, and reporter.
  • Let me save a list of names in this browser so I don't paste it every time.
Make it fun
  • Make the shuffle dramatic: names spin and land one at a time, with a drumroll.
Look inside (no AI needed)
  • Press Play, then Show the code. Find GROUP_NAMES, change them to your school's mascots, and press Run my changes.

What Google Sites does to pasted code

Google Sites shows it inside a frame on your page. That keeps your site safe, and it sets the limits: the page can't remember anything between visits, so saved settings and scores vanish; it can't reach the rest of your site or your visitors' accounts; sound and full screen wait for a tap; and a very long page may be refused or load slowly. It shows only as big as you drag the frame.

Cut off, or misbehaving? Hand your AI this, in the same chat:

Promptwords: paste them into the same chat

Make this one self-contained HTML file for a Google Sites embed box about 800 pixels wide: no outside libraries, fonts, or images; nothing saved between visits; no sound until a tap; large type that works on a phone; and as short as it can be while keeping everything it does.

No Site yet? Go to sites.google.com and choose Blank site. You can also put a starter on a Site as it is: press Play, then Show the code, select it all, copy, and paste it into the Embed code tab. The full steps, and what to do when Sites refuses it: Three levels, level 2.

Behind the scenes

Scripts: code that does a job in your Google Drive. Nothing to play. You paste a script into Apps Script, a tool built into every Google account, press Run, and approve what it may touch.

What level 3 teaches
  1. 1Copy the scriptIt's code, not a prompt. Paste your folder's or Doc's address into its settings line first, and the script carries it.
  2. 2Paste it into Apps ScriptA new project at script.google.com, or inside your Sheet with Extensions, Apps Script. Not your AI chat.
  3. 3Run it, and allowChoose its name beside Run, press Run, read what it asks to touch, and allow.
  4. 4Open what it madeThe Execution log's last line links the Sheet or folder it made.

Standalone on its own

A project of its own, started at script.google.com, that shows up among your files in Drive. It reaches a file by its ID, and it's yours alone until you share it. The Drive Inventory is one.

Bound inside a file

Started from inside a Sheet, Doc, or Slides file with Extensions, Apps Script, and saved in it, not as a file of its own. It can use the file it lives in without an ID, everyone who can edit the file can edit the script, and a copy of the file carries a copy of the script. The Doc Multiplier is one.

The Drive Inventory

For curriculum leads and anyone with a deep folder

Point it at a folder. It reads every folder inside and writes a Sheet: each document, its type, its link, and when it last changed.

Monday: a whole curriculum folder, one search away.

Standalone script · reads one folder · about an hour the first time

Remix menu

Each line copies with the script, as a request for your AI to change it.

Make it useful
  • Add a column for who owns each file.
  • Skip anything that hasn't changed in two years, and list those in a second tab instead.
  • Make the Link column a clickable "Open" instead of the whole address.
Make it careful
  • Explain exactly which permission each line needs, and whether any line could change or delete a file. (It shouldn't.)

The Doc Multiplier

For anyone who makes the same Doc many times

One template Doc and a Sheet of names. From inside the Sheet, it makes a copy of the Doc for each row, named for the row, gathers them in a folder, and writes each link back into the Sheet.

Monday: a planning doc for every team, made in one press.

Bound script, inside your Sheet · a Doc and a Sheet · about an hour the first time

Remix menu

Each line copies with the script, as a request for your AI to change it.

Make it useful
  • Replace the words TEAM and DATE inside each copy with the row's name and today's date.
  • Skip a row if column B already has a link, so I can run it again safely.
  • Add a menu to this Sheet called Multiplier, with one item that runs multiply, so I can run it from the Sheet without opening Apps Script.
Make it careful
  • Before anything is copied, show me how many copies it's about to make and stop if it's more than 30.
