Every starter here already works, and each one is useful on Monday. Play one, hand it to your AI, then make it yours. This is level 1, in the chat. Picked level 2 or 3? Your example is on the levels page.

For anyone who runs a meeting

The Meeting Timer

Your agenda on screen with a big countdown, a gentle chime when it's time to move on, and a parking lot.

Monday: run your next PLC with it on the screen.

Remix menu
Make it yours
  • Change the agenda to my meeting: ___ (10 minutes), ___ (15 minutes), ___ (5 minutes).
  • Show our team norms along the bottom: ___.
  • Use our school colors: ___ and ___.
Make it useful
  • Add a protocol mode: 5 minutes of silent writing, then 10 minutes of discussion, with a different chime for each.
  • Add a spinner that picks who takes notes and who keeps time.
  • Add a button that copies the parking lot as a list I can paste into our notes.
Make it fun
  • When time runs out, play a game-show buzzer instead of the chime.
  • If we finish the agenda early, celebrate with confetti.
Look inside (no AI needed)
  • Press Play, then Show the code. Find AGENDA, change the minutes for one item, and press Run my changes.

For teachers, coaches, and anyone leading a workshop

The Group Maker

Paste names, pick a size, and shuffle. It spreads your helpers out and keeps apart anyone who shouldn't be together.

Monday: tomorrow's lab groups, made in a second.

Remix menu
Make it yours
  • Use these group names instead: ___.
  • Make the group cards big enough to read on a projector from the back of the room.
Make it useful
  • Add a box where I can type pairs who should be kept apart.
  • Give each group roles: facilitator, recorder, timekeeper, and reporter.
  • Let me save a list of names in this browser so I don't paste it every time.
Make it fun
  • Make the shuffle dramatic: names spin and land one at a time, with a drumroll.
Look inside (no AI needed)
  • Press Play, then Show the code. Find GROUP_NAMES, change them to your school's mascots, and press Run my changes.
  • About names: names typed into the running tool stay on your computer. Names pasted into an AI chat go to that company. Today, stick with the made-up ones.

For leaders and anyone who juggles calendars

The Calendar Explorer

Every calendar in one view you can filter and search, with busy days and double-booked rooms flagged.

Monday: spot next month's collisions before they happen.

Remix menu
Make it yours

Make it useful
  • Add a month grid view with a colored dot for each event.

Just for fun

The Teacher's Lounge Oracle

Ask a question. Get a dramatic, slightly ridiculous prophecy, with a gong.

Monday: a vocabulary oracle or a discussion-question picker, if you remix it.

Remix menu
Make it silly
  • Make the Oracle talk like an over-excited game-show host.
  • Add a drumroll before the gong, and confetti when the prophecy appears.
  • Write 10 new prophecies about my school, ___. Keep them kind.
Make it yours
  • Change the colors to my school colors: ___ and ___.
  • Add a button that reads the prophecy out loud.
  • Teach the Oracle a new topic: when I ask about ___, it should answer with prophecies about ___.
Make it useful
  • Turn it into a vocabulary oracle. Each spin reveals one of these words and what it means: ___.
  • Turn it into a discussion-question picker for my class on ___.
Look inside (no AI needed)
  • Press Play, then Show the code. Find SPIN_SECONDS, change the 3 to a 10, and press Run my changes.
  • In the code, find the coffee topic under TOPICS. Add your own prophecy in quotes, end it with a comma, press Run my changes, and ask about coffee.

Where do I paste it?

Gemini

Open gemini.google.com. In the prompt bar, choose Canvas (look in the + menu or under Tools), then paste.

Claude

Open claude.ai and paste. The starter appears beside the chat as an artifact. Nothing there? Turn on artifacts in Settings › Capabilities.

ChatGPT

Open chatgpt.com and paste. When the code appears, switch on Preview on the code block.

Microsoft Copilot

In a Microsoft district? Signed in with a school account, Copilot Pages can build and preview small apps too.
