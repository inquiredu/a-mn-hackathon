Grant a wish

Now build something from nothing, at your level. It's the same loop you just used, minus the training wheels. It starts with you, the AI does the heavy lifting, and it comes back to you. Level 2 or 3? Your level's prompt and steps work for a wish too: a different document, a different folder, a different job.

  1. 1

    Say what you wish for

    Fill in three short answers. The wish writes itself. No wish yet? Take one from the jar.

    Your wish, ready to paste:

  2. 2

    Hand it to your AI

    Paste it into Gemini (choose Canvas first), Claude, or ChatGPT (switch on Preview when the code appears). A first version usually shows up in under a minute.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    Ask for one change at a time. "Make the buttons bigger." "Add a sound when I win." "Use our school colors."

  4. 4

    Break it, fix it

    Something will go sideways. Tell the AI exactly what you see: "When I press Start, nothing happens."

  5. 5

    Peek under the hood

    Explain three lines of this code to me like I've never coded before. Pick the three most interesting ones.

  6. 6

    Back to you

    Is it what you wished for? Who would you show it to? Pin it to the gallery wall.

Stuck? Three moves.

  • Describe it. Tell the AI exactly what you see and what you expected instead.
  • Rewind. "Go back to the version before that one."
  • Ask for help. In your breakout room, press Meet's Ask for help button. A host gets the message and will pop into your room.

Pin it to the gallery wall

Got something working, even halfway? Put a picture of it on the wall, and link the picture to what you made. Half-built counts. Leaving early? Pin it first, and it stays up for Show & Cheer.

1. Copy a link to your work

Gemini

At the top right of the canvas, choose Share & export, then Share, then Copy link.

Claude

Open the artifact and choose Publish, then copy the link. On a school or work plan, choose Share, then Share & copy link.

ChatGPT

At the top of the chat, choose Share, then Create link or Copy link. The link opens the conversation, code and all.

Microsoft Copilot

On your page, choose Share at the top right, then Copy link.

A link from a school account may open only for people in your district. The picture in step 2 shows your work to everyone either way. Each tool's own help on sharing.

2. Take a picture of it

Windows

Press Windows + Shift + S and drag a box around your work. It's copied, ready to paste.

Mac

Press Command + Control + Shift + 4 and drag a box around your work. It's copied, ready to paste.

Chromebook

Press Ctrl + Shift + Show windows, drag a box around your work, and choose Capture. It's copied, ready to paste.

3. Put it on the wall

  1. Open the gallery wall and click a slide with room on it. There are three; when one fills up, use the next.
  2. Paste: Ctrl + V, or Command + V on a Mac.
  3. Drag a corner to make it postcard-size, and move it to an open spot.
  4. With the picture still selected, press Ctrl + K (Command + K on a Mac), paste your link, and choose Apply.

Now anyone can click your picture to open what you made. The wall is open to anyone with its link, and so is a published link, so keep to made-up data.
